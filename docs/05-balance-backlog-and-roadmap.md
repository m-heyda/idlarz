## 1. Balance leftovers (each needs approval before changing)

| # | Topic | Observation | Idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BL1 | **Gap between active and idle players is small** (hardcore ≈ 32 d, idle ≈ 40 d in the sim) | Gold is only part of the economy; the castle timers (5 d 5 h to level 6) and resource costs dominate | Raise click power further, or let clicks also pay a small share of other resources / tap yields; re-run the sim |
| BL2 | **Levels and stat points barely matter** | ~1 point/level, +2.5 % each on a pool that the castle multiplies | Gate castle levels by player level, or raise per-point effects, or add respec |
| BL3 | **Chests and tile taps are small** next to income | chest = 10–60 min of *passive* gold; tap = 0.5 s of one tile | Scale chests with the click-inclusive income or give resource packs |
| BL4 | **Meat/food is still the strongest engine** (animal stacking × castle multiplier) | animals dominate mid-game income in the sim traces | Revisit `costScaling` (1.5–1.8) or add upkeep once the unfinished systems exist |
| BL5 | **Resource sinks are thin after the castle and tiles** | wood/stone/coal beyond tile and castle needs only sell for 0.5–1 gold | Equipment upgrades (U2), buildings with upgrade levels (U13), storage caps tied to the castle |
| BL6 | **Merchant has little depth** | flat prices for resources and bag items, no saturation; bonuses, supplies and a shuffling gear shelf (30 min or paid refresh) exist now | optional "market saturation" (price drops with volume sold, recovers over ~30 min) if burst selling shows up again |
| BL7 | **Late tiles are very expensive** | since the early-game pace pass tile 50 costs ~3.6 K, tile 100 ~105 K and tile 400 ~1.6 M gold (soft ×1.007 steps to 300, then `(n/300)^4.5`) | fine as an endgame sink; revisit if the Ember discount (cap 45 %) makes late rebirth runs too cheap |
| BL8 | **Idle tile income** | with `BIOME_YIELD.scale` = 1.3 a tile adds ~0.1–0.5 gold-equivalent/s | consider per-biome specialisation bonuses (e.g. +x % per 4 same biome) |
| BL9 | **Ember shop is six items** | all of them are flat percentages, so the choice is mostly "which first" | add one or two with a different shape (e.g. longer chest timer cap, extra critter gifts, a free animal slot) |
| BL10 | **Late Ember runs flatten** | gain grows with `sqrt(tiles)` and the castle level, the shop is capped at 10 levels each; once the shop is maxed points only add the +5 % income each | consider a second prestige tier or a sink for surplus points |
| BL11 | **Biome luck and the 24 h player** | after the Coast/water clustering merge the seed spread is large (hardcore 27–45 d) and the 24 h/day auto-clicker bot is ~25 % faster than the 8 h/day hardcore bot | tune `BIOME_SPAWN` weights (fewer water tiles early or a wood/stone-yielding coast) and re-run `sim --seeds=3` |

| BL12 | **Combat rewards and difficulty** | kill gold / XP / hide are `rewardSec` seconds of income (level 1: 90 s) with 15-minute spawns; the sim's `human` profiles now fight a rat every 900 s and the King every 3 h on a gear schedule (`GEAR_SCHEDULE` in `scripts/sim.ts`, from fight-sim.md), which is about +20 % gold; the schedule is an assumption until real playtests exist | add a fighting bot to `sim.ts`, check the share of income from fights stays small, tune `COMBAT.spawn`, `MONSTERS.rat.rewardSec`, armor `K` |
| BL13 | **Round 2 numbers are first guesses** | the ten newer buildings (each ~0.15-0.45 total output), the chest reward table (50 / 25 / 12 / 10 / 3 %), the unlock thresholds (Swamp 6, Tundra 10) and the cluster chances were set by feel | run `npm run sim`, then tune `BUILDINGS`, `CHEST.rewards`, `BIOME_RULES` (one config each) |
| BL14 | **Reserved resources** | iron, fish, mana, blue crystal, sapphire, ability shard and the random-item box exist as icons only (`RESERVED_RESOURCES`); the green gem is live as the rebirth currency (Embers; the blue diamond is the separate premium currency, Gems); nothing produces, sells or spends them | design them as the end-game / rebirth currencies (e.g. mana for abilities, iron for gear crafting, fish for a fishing mechanic) and add them to `Resources` + the merchant + `sanitizeSave` together |
| BL15 | **Free starting ring speeds up the opening** | 8 free tiles produce from second 0: in the sim (hardcore) tiles10 comes after 0.5 h instead of 1.0 h, tiles16 2.6 h vs 4.6 h, castle 2 at 11.7 h vs 17.2 h, tiles30 1.9 d vs 2.4 d | if too fast: scale the ring's yield (a `STARTER_YIELD` factor in `calculateResourceRates`), lower the start gold, or raise `SCALING_CONFIG.growthEarly` |
| BL16 | **Late tile price and map scale (rebirth-centred)** | the map has no edge and is limited only by `COORD_LIMIT` (±16,000) and `MAX_OWNED_TILES` (250,000); it renders and saves at that size (see docs/04 for the measured costs). The price is the old exponential up to 100 tiles, then `(n/100)^2.5` (`TILE_PRICE`), XP per tile has a similar tail after 300 (`XP_TILE`), `RESOURCE_CAP` is 1e21; tile 1,000 ≈ 4e13 gold, tile 10,000 ≈ 1.4e16, tile 50,000 ≈ 8e17. These are placeholders: rebirth is a basic MVP and will later give more starting resources and want much bigger maps | when rebirth is designed: pick the intended map size per rebirth tier, then move `tailFrom` / `tailExponent` (and the `TILE_RESOURCE_COST` shares) to hit it; re-run `npm run sim` and `npm run report` (the report prints a late-tile table). Also check: building price (`BUILDING_COST`, clamped finite but exponential), per-tile income vs price at 1k+ tiles, the XP/level curve at 10k+ tiles, float precision of gold past 2^53 |
| BL17 | **Big-map polish** | the minimap, the highlight and the unbounded map are in; measured at 50,000 tiles panning is smooth with an occasional 33-60 ms frame (docs/04 C) | lighter component (or event delegation) for plain explored tiles, incremental income on a purchase, chunk drawing in a worker (OffscreenCanvas), a 0.5 zoom step or a larger minimap, workshop / building filters on the minimap, and "go to" for the castle / a workshop |

## 2. Framework upgrade notes

On Next 15.5.28, React 19.3, Tailwind 4.3, Zustand 5.0, TypeScript 5.9, ESLint 9.39 (all within their majors); linting runs through the ESLint CLI (`npm run lint`) and the workspace root is pinned in `next.config.ts`. Held back deliberately: **Next 16**, **TypeScript 7**, **ESLint 10**, `@types/node` 26.

- Next 16 makes Turbopack the default, needs Node ≥ 20.9, and fixes the two PostCSS advisories bundled inside Next 15 (build-time tooling, not shipped to players).
- TypeScript 7 and ESLint 10: check `typescript-eslint` / `eslint-config-next` peer ranges first; upgrade one at a time and keep `npm run build`, `npm test` and `npm run sim` green.
