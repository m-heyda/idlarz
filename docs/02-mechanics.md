# 02 · Mechanics

Exact rules as implemented. Constants live in [`src/config/gameConfig.ts`](../src/config/gameConfig.ts); formulas and tables are in [03](03-formulas-and-scaling.md). The in-game tutorial (`Tutorial.tsx`) is generated from the same constants, so it always matches this document.

## 1. The loop in one paragraph

You start with a castle and its eight neighbouring tiles on an unbounded map, and 125 gold. Gold, other resources and XP trickle in every second. Gold (plus wood/stone/coal later) buys **adjacent unexplored tiles**, each of which becomes a biome and adds income. The **castle** is a big timed upgrade that multiplies everything. **Farm animals** turn food into meat, the **merchant** converts anything into gold (and back, at a loss), **clicking the map** and **chests** add active bonuses, every **level** gives a stat point, and **monsters** on your land can be fought for XP, gold and gear (§15, automatic fights run by the server). First finish line = castle 6, best click power and ~40+ tiles (about 4 weeks for an active player, [06](06-playtest-and-pacing-report.md)). After that the game continues with castle levels 7–10 and **Rebirth** (§13), which restarts the map for permanent Ember bonuses.

## 2. Map & buying tiles

- The map has no edge: castle at (25, 25) and its 8 neighbours owned from the start (see *Starting land* below), tiles can be bought in any direction, also to negative coordinates (limits: ±16,000 tiles from the origin and 250,000 explored tiles). Drag to pan, mouse wheel to zoom (four fixed steps), **right-click an explored tile to build on it** (see Buildings). Panning is limited to the explored land: the middle of the screen can go 6 tiles past its edge, so a long line of tiles bought to the left can be followed to its end (zoom out to find it), but there is no scrolling into endless emptiness. The **minimap** (bottom right, "Show minimap" in the global menu) shows every explored tile as a coloured square with a gold dot on the castle and purple dots on workshops, **red dots for monsters** and **yellow, softly glowing dots for treasure chests** (dots that would overlap merge into one bigger dot: 3 px for one, 5 for 2-4, 7 for 5-9, 9 for 10 or more; a small legend in the corner counts them), outlines the part on screen and moves the view when clicked or dragged. When the castle is off screen an **arrow on the edge of the screen** points toward it (with the distance in tiles) and a **Back to castle** button appears under the header; either one centres the view on the castle.
- A tile can be bought only if it is **4-neighbour adjacent** to an owned tile and you can pay its whole cost: gold, plus **extra resources from larger territories** (`TILE_RESOURCE_COST`: wood 30 % and stone 25 % of the gold from the 15th tile, coal 20 % from 30, food 10 % from 40 and **hide 3 % from 120**, because milk and eggs are kept for other uses; tiles are counted as the **economy count**, see below; gold stays the biggest single part). Charisma discounts every part of the cost (0.5 %/point, max 25 %). The hover card shows all costs, green when affordable.
- Cost curve (tuned so ~100 tiles come within the first day and 200-300 within 2-3 days): ×1.13 per owned tile up to 15 tiles, ×1.07 up to 100, ×1.007 per tile up to 300, then a steeper polynomial tail (`(n/300)^4.5`: tile 400 costs ~1.6 M, tile 500 ~4 M, tile 1,000 ~96 M gold), capped far below the resource cap, so the map never stops being buyable by price but every further 100 tiles takes clearly longer ([03 §1](03-formulas-and-scaling.md#1-tile-cost), [06](06-playtest-and-pacing-report.md), roadmap BL16).
- Not enough → shake + `wrong` sound. Success → `purchase` sound, XP, +100 reputation.
- **Biome assignment**: a purchase turns into a biome by the **placement rules** below, rolled when you buy (there is no biome picker any more: the old "every 4th tile you choose" step is gone). The cost is charged at once.
- Name prompt + tutorial at exactly 2 owned tiles while the name is still `Explorer`.

### Starting land and the economy count

A new game (and every rebirth) starts with the castle **and its 8 neighbours owned**. The ring is generated from the basic biomes (`BIOME_RULES.startRing`): plains 5 / forest 3 by weight (never hills or lake, so the castle sits on gentle land), each ring tile continues the previous one 50 % of the time, so the ring forms a few patches; at least 3 plains and never more than 4 forest. These 8 tiles are free (`GameState.freeTiles` = 9; an old save keeps 1).

Tile cost, the extra resource costs, XP per tile and the rebirth requirements all use the **economy count** `economyTiles = owned − freeTiles + 1` (purchases + the castle), so the first purchase still costs 55 gold and the curve is unchanged. Statistics, monster spawning and the map use the real owned count. A save is checked for this (`freeTiles` is 1, or 9 with the whole ring owned).

### Biome placement rules (`src/config/biomeRules.ts`, logic in `src/utils/biomeRules.ts`)

All numbers live in one config file; the rules apply to new purchases only (existing maps are not touched). "Purchased" = tiles bought so far.

1. **Unlocks.** Plains, Forest, Hills and Lake are available from the start. **Swamp** after 6 purchases, **Tundra** after 10. **Coast** only ever beside water (rule 3).
2. **Conflicts** (checked against all 8 neighbours that are owned): **Tundra ↔ Swamp**, **Tundra ↔ Coast** and **Hills ↔ Coast** may never touch. **Plains is neutral** and always fits, so a purchase always has at least one biome to become.
3. **Coast needs a Lake or Coast** among the 8 neighbours.
4. **Clustering (random purchases).** The candidates are the unlocked biomes the rules allow. First, with a chance of `base + 10 × (extra same-biome 4-neighbours)` (capped at 90 %), the tile **copies** one of its 4-neighbour biomes that is a candidate. Base chances: Forest 60, Hills 55, Lake 50, Swamp 55, Tundra 65, Plains 45, Coast 40. Otherwise it rolls by the old weights (`BIOME_SPAWN`: every biome 1, Coast 0.6, water likelier beside water).

| Biome | Unlocks | May not touch | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plains | start | - | - |
| Forest, Lake | start | - | - |
| Hills | start | Coast | - |
| Swamp | 6 purchases | Tundra | - |
| Tundra | 10 purchases | Swamp, Coast | - |
| Coast | start | Tundra, Hills | Lake or Coast nearby |

## 3. Biomes, adjacency, buildings

| Biome | Output / s (before adjacency & modifiers) |
| --- | --- |
| Castle (unique) | gold 0.1, wood 0.05, stone 0.1, food 0.05, xp 0.5 (not scaled) |
| Forest | gold 0.13, wood 0.39 |
| Plains | gold 0.13, food 0.39 (+5 % meat modifier per Plains tile) |
| Hills | gold 0.065, stone 0.39, coal 0.26 |
| Swamp | food 0.13, wood 0.26 |
| Tundra | gold 0.13, coal 0.455 |
| Lake | gold 0.065, food 0.325 |
| Coast | gold 0.13, food 0.26 |

(Config values × `BIOME_YIELD.scale` = 1.3.) **Adjacency**: each owned same-biome 4-neighbour adds +25 % to that tile's output, including its building.

**Buildings** (right-click build menu): **right-click any explored tile** (not the castle, not a lake, and not while a chest or a monster is on it; hovering shows "Right-click to build here") to open a menu with all 16 buildings, their output, the price of the next one and each building drawn on a patch of the tile's own biome. Click one to build it: **one permanent building per tile**; the tile keeps its biome and its output and the building adds its own on top (same x1.3 yield scale and adjacency bonus). (The Grounds biome, the old building lot, was removed: saves that had Grounds tiles turn each into the land biome around it when they load, with the building and chest kept.) Buildings **cost resources** (base 500 gold, 250 wood, 150 stone) and **every building costs more than the last**: the first four are cheap (x0.4, x0.6, x0.8, x1.0 of the base), then x1.35 per building for the next 12, x1.25 for the next 16 and x1.15 after that (the same three-stage shape as tile prices; building 5 = 675 gold, 10 = 3.0K, 16 = 18.3K, 20 = 44.7K, 32 = 651K, 40 = 2.0M; wood and stone scale the same way, the Thrift shop upgrade lowers it). Output (config values, x1.3 yield scale plus adjacency): Farm food 0.2 · Mine stone/coal 0.2 · Lumber Mill wood 0.2 · Market gold 0.2 · Blacksmith 0.07 of all five · Workshop xp 0.2 · Lumber Camp wood 0.18 · Quarry stone 0.25 · Fishing Hut food 0.16 + gold 0.05 · Hunter's Lodge meat 0.15 · Tavern food 0.12 + xp 0.08 · Tower stone 0.08 + xp 0.12 · Well food 0.15 · Alchemy Lab xp 0.12 + gold 0.08 · Stone Craft stone 0.15 + gold 0.08 · Barracks gold 0.1 + xp 0.1. Every output is an existing resource; the ten newer buildings are first-pass numbers (see BL13 in [05](05-balance-backlog-and-roadmap.md)). The Hunter's Lodge is the only source of meat besides the Farm's animals.

**Foundation**: a building on any biome keeps that biome's ground and gets a small worked-ground **pad** in the style of that biome under it (plains: packed earth and cobbles, forest: leaf litter, hills: gravel, swamp: plank courses over mud, tundra: trampled snow, coast: sand with a plank landing), so a farm in a forest stands in a clearing and one on tundra stands in snow; building tiles carry no trees or rocks. Ambient critters on a building tile are those of its biome. The sprite is 48×60 and its roof rises over the tile above.

## 4. Resources

Gold, wood, stone, coal (drawn as charcoal "fuel"), food, meat, the farm products **eggs, milk and hide**, and xp. All except gold/xp can be sold to the merchant; wood, stone, coal, food and meat can also be bought (at 2× the sell price); the farm products are only earned. The header shows every currency and material except xp (it is the level bar) as a small icon chip, in one boxed group per **tier** of the resource catalog (`tier` field in `src/catalog/resources.ts`): **Currency** (gold, gems, Embers), **T0 base materials** (wood, stone, coal, food, meat) and **T1 materials** (eggs, milk, hide). Each chip shows its icon and a small amount (*Show compact header* in the cog menu leaves only the icons); hovering a strip (or clicking it, which pins the list) lists every resource of the tier with its exact-ish amount and its production in the unit that reads best (per second from 1/s, per minute from 1/min, else per hour). The catalog keeps the long texts (description, used for, comes from, merchant pitch) for the merchant and a later encyclopedia; the header does not show them. No stockpile can exceed 10²¹ (`RESOURCE_CAP`). The icons come from a supplied pixel-art sheet (cut out by `scripts/pixel/extract_sheet.py`, 40×40, twinkle strips for the shiny ones). **Embers** (the green gem) are the rebirth currency, see §13, and **Gems** (the blue diamond) the premium currency; neither is a `Resources` key (Embers live in the prestige state, Gems are their own server-held balance). **Reserved art** (not resources yet, nothing produces or spends them): iron, fish, mana, blue crystal, sapphire, ability shard and the random-item box exist as icons (`RESERVED_RESOURCES` in `pixelIcons.ts`) for a later end-game / rebirth system ([05](05-balance-backlog-and-roadmap.md) BL14).

## 5. Castle

- Max level 10. **Each upgrade is a timed build**: resources are paid up front, then the castle works for 1 h, 4 h, 12 h, 36 h and 72 h (levels 2–6, 5 d 5 h in total), then 3 d, 4 d, 5 d and 7 d for levels 7–10 (24 d 5 h in total). Master Builders (Ember shop) shortens these times. It keeps its current bonus meanwhile and only one upgrade runs at a time. The timer is advanced by the game tick (never by the system clock), is saved, and is shown in the castle dialog and its tooltip.
- Effect of level L: the modifier of every resource doubles each level up to 6 (+20 % … +640 %), then grows by a flat +320 % per level (+960 % at 7 … +1920 % at 10), plus **+0.5 XP/s per level above 1** (flat). The castle sprite glows brighter from level 6 on, and level 10 gets sparks and the gold border.
- **Fortress (levels 7-10)**: from level 7 the castle grows over its whole 3x3 bonus area. The violet area marker is replaced by stone **walls** along the four edges, a **gatehouse** in the middle of the south wall and a **tower on every corner**; the keep itself is a detailed animated castle on its own (cobbled) tile that spills a few pixels over its neighbours. All eight surrounding biomes stay visible. The towers get richer every level (7 red cone roof, 8 slate roof with crown and banner, 9 gold roof with glowing slits, 10 crystal spire and two banners) and the walls turn gold-trimmed from level 9. The area is a **ward**: no monsters or chests spawn inside it, and monsters already standing there when level 7 is reached are driven out (a monster you are fighting stays until the fight ends).
- **Castle range (violet area)**: 3x3 up to level 7, then it grows by one tile on every side with each level: **5x5 at level 8, 7x7 at level 9, 9x9 at level 10** (`CASTLE_RANGE`, `castleRangeRadius`). From level 8 the walls and towers stay on the inner 3x3 and the violet area is drawn around them; the ward stays the 3x3 too. **Area bonus** (`CASTLE_AREA_BONUS`, `castleAreaBonus`): every tile inside the range, and the building on it, produces extra output: **+50 % (x1.5) at levels 1-6** (the 3x3, no walls yet), then from level 7 (walls) its output once more for every tile the range reaches out: **+100 % at level 7, +200 % at 8, +300 % at 9, +400 % at 10** (the castle tile itself included). It is part of the base income (the Statistics window lists it as *Castle area*), so the global modifiers apply on top; tiles inside the range show it in their tooltip and the castle tile's tooltip explains it. The tiles in range also get gold sparkles; the castle dialog shows the next size ("Castle range 3x3 -> 5x5").
- Costs: see [03 §4](03-formulas-and-scaling.md#4-castle) (gold, wood, stone, coal from level 2→3, food + meat from 3→4; levels 7–10 cost about 1.6× the previous step).

## 6. Farm

- Each purchase adds **one more animal** of that kind (production = base × count). First cost in food: chicken 1 K, deer 10 K, pig 60 K, cow 400 K; each next one of the same kind costs ×1.3 / 1.33 / 1.36 / 1.4 more, so the first chicken is within reach after a few minutes and a full pen is a goal for days. The pen holds **12 animals per kind** (more with Pen Expansion); a save that already has more keeps them.
- Every animal also yields a **farm product**: chickens eggs (0.03/s), cows milk (0.225/s), deer and pigs hide (0.0375 / 0.075 per s). Sold, a product is worth about a third of the animal's meat.
- Meat and product income = `Σ production × upgrades × (1 + castle modifier + 0.05 × Plains tiles for meat)`.
- **Upgrades** (bought in the Farm window with resources, 10 levels, the pen 8): Feed Trough +5 % meat, Hen House +15 % eggs, Dairy +15 % milk, Tannery +15 % hide, Pen Expansion +4 animals per kind and +2 % all farm output per level. Each costs ×1.6–1.9 more per level; the product upgrades are paid partly in the product itself.
- **The window** is one meadow filling the whole dialog with the pen and its animals in it (animals are scenery, there is nothing to click on them). The **animal shop** is a panel along the right edge (one card per kind: owned/limit, output per animal, Buy ×1 / ×10 / Max; hovering a card lights up that kind in the pen), a corner readout (fixed width, so ticking numbers never move it; it folds up to its title) shows the output and stock, and the upgrade shelf sits along the bottom. The shop and the shelf can be collapsed too. Animals spread out over the meadow instead of bunching up.
- While the Farm window is open a critter wanders by every 60–120 s (one at a time, 10 s lifetime; the first after 45 s). It is decoration only: it cannot be clicked and gives nothing.

## 7. Merchant

- Sell: wood 0.75, stone 0.9, coal 1.1, food 0.4, meat 1, eggs 2, milk 3, hide 4 gold per unit (`floor(amount × price × Haggler)`); buying costs twice that. The four basic prices follow how fast each resource is produced early (measured on random 10-40 tile maps): the slower it comes in, the more it sells for, so an hour of any of them pays about the same, and the early total sell value is where it was (before: wood 0.5, stone 0.5, coal 1, food 0.75).
- Buy resources (wood, stone, coal, food, meat; the **Resources** tab is one table, one row per resource, with an *Amount* picker on top - 1 / 10 / 100 / 25 % / 50 % / Max - shared by every Sell and Buy button, and the gold a click pays or costs on its hover card): 2× the sell price, rounded **up** (`buyCost`, shared by the store and the tab). Buying then selling (or the reverse) can never gain gold, even with a maxed Haggler — covered by tests.
- **Tabs**: *Resources* (sell and buy resources), *Goods* (one compact shelf of gear and potions, selling from your bag), *Upgrades* (click power and the merchant bonuses), plus the placeholders *Quests* and *Gambling* ([04](04-unfinished-and-known-issues.md)). The window opens on Resources.
- **Goods**: a shelf of **six pieces of gear**, shown on wooden planks of small bag-style slots (four to a plank, CSS only) together with the always-in-stock potions (Minor and Greater Healing Potion: both are in the shelf's fixed order, which a new stock or a Refresh shuffles again, `shelfCells` in `lib/merchant.ts`; a Buy button sits under each slot and its price shows when you hover it) - the gear is rolled by the loot pipeline (source `merchant`, profile `merchant_stock`): **common 60 % / uncommon 40 %, nothing better**, built around the castle level but held to the gear you wear: the item level is centred at most 3 above the **average item level of your worn pieces** and never more than **5 above it** (`MERCHANT_GOODS`, `shelfLevels` in `lib/merchant.ts`). It is **reshuffled after 30 minutes of play time** (a countdown is shown) or at once for gold (**Refresh**: 5k, then 5k more each time up to 50k for the tenth, then 50k more each time: 100k, 150k ...; counted per run and saved as `merchantRefreshes`, a rebirth starts it again). Every piece costs **5× the gold the merchant would pay for it** (`shopPriceOf`), so buying and selling can never gain gold, even with a maxed Haggler. Buying takes the piece off the shelf; a full bag buys nothing; an empty shelf waits for the next shuffle. The shelf is saved (full item validation on load) and starts again on a rebirth. **Supplies** below the shelf are always in stock: Minor Healing Potion 80, Greater 360.
- **Sell items** (Goods tab, under the shelf): your bag is listed. Click an item to sell one, Shift-click for the stack, or "Sell common" / "Sell up to uncommon" to clear plain gear (potions and worn gear are never touched). An item sells for its rarity's value (common 300, uncommon 1200, rare 3600, epic 12000, legendary 45000 at item level 1) times the item-level curve (x1.3 per level up to 10, x1.12 above), the quality roll (x0.75 to x1.25) and every affix (+15 % to +50 % each); potions stay flat (20 / 90). Refused during a fight. Organize is a bag function: use the button on the Character bag or the right-click menu (see §16).
- **Upgrades** tab: **Click power** (gold per click as a % of your gold/s): 50 % → 90 % (750 K) → 130 % (2.5 M) → 180 % (10 M); **bonuses** with level ladders (price ×1.9–3 per level), in three groups. *Economy*: *Haggler* +3 % gold from everything you sell (10 levels), *Bulk Discount* −1 % tile and building cost (8, shares the 45 % cap with Charisma and Thrift), *Lucky Charm* +2 Luck (10), *Pack Rat* +4 bag slots (4), *Foreman* +1 % income from every resource but XP (10), *Skinner* +5 % meat and hide from the animals (8). *Combat*: *Quick Hands* +2 % attack speed, *Keen Eye* +1 % crit chance, *Deadly Aim* +1 % super-crit chance, *Might* +2 % physical and *Arcana* +2 % magic damage (8 levels each), *Vampiric Fang* +0.5 % life leech and *Second Wind* +0.25 HP/s regeneration (6 each); the caps (crit 75 %, super crit 50 %, leech 15 %) still hold, and the server's fights use them from the last accepted save (`merchantCombat`, `playerProfile`). *Rebirth*: *Ancestral Tithe* +5 % Embers from a rebirth (10). The ladders are the late gold sink: the first levels cost 0.25-0.6 M, but all of them together cost over a billion, far more than a player can spare before the first rebirth (`tests/merchantUpgrades.test.ts`), so they are bought over several runs. **All of them start again on a rebirth**, except Pack Rat (the bag stays). The old fixed gear shop is gone: gear comes from the shuffling shelf.

## 8. Clicking

- A **real click on the map** pays `gold/s × click power`. A click on an owned tile also taps it: `0.5 s` of that tile's output (each tile has a 1.2 s cooldown).
- Clicks on menus, the header, buttons and dialogs never pay. Script-dispatched (untrusted) clicks never pay.
- **Rate limit** (token buckets, shared by gold click and tile tap): up to 8 clicks in a burst, 5 clicks/s sustained, plus a long-run allowance (20 000 clicks bank, refilling 1.5 clicks/s ≈ 130 K paid clicks/day). A fast human is never limited; an auto-clicker is capped at roughly a dedicated human ([07](07-security-and-exploits.md)).

## 9. Chests

A chest appears every 10 min (the timer only runs while monsters and chests together fill less than 30 % of the free tiles - see Spawning). **Where** (`pickChestSpot`): any owned tile except the castle, a building, a monster, another chest and (from castle level 7) the castle's ward; each tile weighs 1, **+3 if it touches land you have not bought**, +1 per 4 tiles of distance from the castle, ×0.3 if another chest is within 2 tiles - so chests lean toward the edge of your land and spread out. It is drawn as a banded wooden chest on a small grass patch (from the supplied sheet) with a soft glow, a bob and an animated lock and lid glint. Chests are saved with the map.

**What is inside** (`rollChestLoot`, chances in `CHEST.rewards`): **gold** 50 % (`passive gold/s × random 10–60 min × (1 + 0.5 % × Luck)`, as before) · **resource bundle** 25 % (1–2 of wood, stone, coal, food, each 10–30 min of its income, at least 25) · **XP** 12 % (10–30 min of XP income, at least 60) · **lucky chest** 10 % (gold ×1.5) · **gear** 8 % (one item into the bag from the `basic_chest` loot profile: gear with a rolled rarity, item level and affixes (82 %) or a potion, now and then a legendary weapon - never Rattatui pieces; the castle level raises the item level; a full bag turns it into gold, and the auto-sell filter sells it on the spot) - see [10-loot-system.md](10-loot-system.md). Each Luck point (character, merchant and gear) shifts 1 % of the gold chance into lucky chests (80 %) and gear (20 %). A toast shows what was found; the last result is not saved.

## 10. XP, levels and stat points

The **Character tab** (called Stats before; the paper doll and bag are the **Equipment** tab) shows the character's real numbers through `effectiveStats` (`lib/effectiveStats.ts`): damage, crit chance and damage, **super crit chance and damage**, armor, dodge, regen, leech, luck, gold find, mana, XP gain, tile discount and the rebirth bonuses, each from every source that applies (attributes, worn gear, merchant bonuses, Embers). It is one framed page: the hero with an unspent-points badge laid over the portrait (so spending points never moves the layout), the five attributes with their + buttons and an overview (level, XP, gold, tiles, rebirths) beside him, then folding sections - Combat, Bonuses, Resource income (per second / minute / hour) and *Where the income comes from* (a row per resource with its pieces and bonuses). Each section's open/closed state is remembered in the browser (`idlarz.section.<id>`).

XP comes from passive income (`total.xp × (1 + xpGainMultiplier)`), from every tile purchase and from what the server pays (kills, chests, absences; all of them with the XP bonus). **The level curve is slow and lifetime**: level and XP carry over a rebirth (§13), so the curve is paced over rebirths. It is written as anchors in `LEVELING` (`config/gameConfig.ts`; `xpToNextLevel` in `utils/gameUtils.ts`), fitted to the XP the sim bot earns (`npm run sim hardcore-rebirth6-x10`; the bot rebirths about every 3-5 days at first, [06](06-playtest-and-pacing-report.md)): level 10 needs about 1.4e5 XP in all (4-5 h), level 30 1e8 (about day 2), the **soft cap 80** 3e9 (its 3rd rebirth, ~day 10), level 90 6.5e9 (5th rebirth) and the **maximum 100** 1.85e10 (10th rebirth, ~day 25); between two anchors each step is a constant factor above the one before. Every level gives **one attribute point, and levels 2-30 give two extra** (`LEVELING.earlyBonus*`: the early points are what speeds the first days up, and levels are slower now). `totalStatPoints(level)` is the total earned (it is also what the save check expects). The XP of a tile is `45 × 1.02^(k-1) × k/4` (§11 formulas).

## 11. Character stats

| Stat | Per point |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | stone & coal +2.5 %; combat: +2 % damage with physical weapons, +0.25 armor, +1 % crit damage |
| Dexterity | wood & food +2.5 %; combat: +0.5 armor, +0.25 % attack speed, +0.5 % crit damage |
| Intelligence | gold +2.5 %, XP production +0.2 %, XP gained +0.2 %; combat: +2.4 % damage with magic weapons (staff, wand) and Heal, +4 max mana, +0.05 mana/s |
| Vitality | wood & food +2.5 %; combat: +0.5 armor (and HP) |
| Charisma | gold & coal +2.5 %, XP production +0.25 %, XP gained +0.25 %, tile cost −0.5 % (max −25 %), Luck +1 (chests); combat: +0.5 % crit damage |

All text in tooltips and the tutorial comes from `CHARACTER_STAT_CONFIG` and `STAT_RULES` (`config/combatConfig.ts`), so the text and the maths cannot drift apart. **Combat bonuses count the points above the starting 5**, so a fresh character plays exactly as before and every attribute point is worth the same everywhere. Magic is worth more than strength on purpose: 2.4 % against 2 % per point (1.2×), and a staff matches a melee weapon's damage per second at the same tier, so a mage ahead by about 7 % at level 30 (25 spare points) also has mana for skills. Physical ATK / Magic ATK in the Stats tab are those two bonus percentages, DEF is the armor the attributes add (Magic DEF is gone: one DEF), MP is the real mana pool and ATK speed feeds `attacksPerSecond`, the speed your weapon swings at in a fight. Reputation is still display-only.

## 12. UI surfaces

Header (one compact row: the hero's **avatar with the level orb** (click: the character screen with its Character, Equipment, Merchant and Farm tabs; hover: name and XP; the class is changed with the *Change class* button of the Equipment tab), the resource catalog's tiers as three boxed strips of small **icons** (Currency | T0 | T1, see §4; hover or click a strip for a list of every resource of that tier with its amount and production; the green gem opens the Rebirth window), a **Rebirth** button (always shown, greyed out until a rebirth is possible; its tooltip lists the requirements with ✓/✗) and the Settings menu; *Show compact header* in the cog menu drops the amount next to each icon, and the strips wrap onto a second row on narrow screens), map with tile hover cards, castle dialog (with timer), right-click build menu (with the next building's cost), Equipment window (slots + bag), combat panel, level-up and chest toasts, loading screen (until the save is loaded), name prompt + tutorial, class picker, dev-only debug panel, and **Save & Backup** (Settings, or press Control five times): autosave status, export / import as one line of text or a file, reset ([07](07-security-and-exploits.md)). Also Settings, Rebirth (rebirth + Ember shop) and the re-openable tutorial. ## 13. Rebirth and Embers

- Available when the castle is level 6 or more and you own at least 100 tiles (and no castle upgrade is running). The **Rebirth** button in the header is always visible but greyed out until all of that holds (hover it for the checklist, `rebirthStatus` in `gameUtils.ts` is the one place the rule lives); then it opens the dialog. The Embers chip opens the same dialog at any time, for the shop.
- **Embers** (the rebirth currency, shown with the green gem icon; earlier called Legacy points, and before that Gems) gained = `floor(4.5 × (castle level − 5) × sqrt(tiles / 10))`, for example castle 6 / 100 tiles = 14, castle 6 / 300 tiles = 24, castle 10 / 100 tiles = 71 ([03 §9](03-formulas-and-scaling.md#9-rebirth-and-embers)). The dialog shows the preview before you confirm.
- **Resets**: map, resources, castle, animals, buildings, click upgrades, chests, monsters and any open fight, the castle timer; HP is refilled. **Keeps**: name, class, play time, all Embers and shop upgrades, settings, your **gear, bag and potions**, and **the character: lifetime XP, the level it gives and the attributes with the points still to spend** (level 80 takes about three rebirths, 100 about ten; the income follows the attributes). The merchant's Ancestral Tithe adds to the points a rebirth gives.
- **Permanent bonus**: +5 % of total income for every Ember *earned* (spending points never lowers income).
- **Shop** (spend Embers, ten levels each, price grows ×1.4 per level): *Thrift* −2 % tile and building cost per level; *Master Builders* −5 % castle build time; *Click Mastery* +10 % click power; *Provisions* start each run with a bundle of gold, wood and stone; *Night Watch* +1 h of away time per level and *Steward* +5 % away income per level ([11](11-offline-and-sealed-rolls.md)). The Thrift discount and Charisma share one cap of 45 %.
- Expected pace (sim, [06](06-playtest-and-pacing-report.md)): the second run reaches castle 6 in about 70 % of the first run's time, later runs get shorter but flatten.

## 14. Settings and ambient life

- **Settings** (header): sound on/off, volume, ambient life on/off, buttons for Save & backup (same as Control ×5) and the tutorial. Stored in the browser (`idlarz-settings`), separate from the game save.
- **Right-click menus** (`ContextMenu`, one component for all of them): the browser's own menu never shows (except inside text fields). A right-click on an explored tile offers, above the global entries, *Highlight this <biome> area* (tints the connected area of that biome around the clicked tile, every tile of it that touches it side by side, with a translucent colour of its own for 3 s; no border; castle excluded), *Build here...* (opens the build window; greyed with "tile in use" while a chest or monster is on it, absent on built tiles and the castle), *Dismiss <monster>...* on a monster and *Open chest* on a chest; a bag item offers *Equip / Drink*, *Sell one*, *Sell all (N)* (a stack of potions; the hint is the Haggler-adjusted gold) and *Discard...*; the global entries end with the two battle switches, **Auto-battle** and **Auto-use potions**, as a section of their own above the view options (the combat window only shows during a fight). Everything else (empty map, unexplored tiles, the page) gets the global menu alone: **Save game, Export game save, Import / backup..., Show minimap** and, in development builds, **Show admin mode**. The gear button in the header opens the same menu as a dropdown, with **Settings** and **Guide** on top (they are not in the right-click menu).
- **Dismiss monster**: removes the monster from the map for good, with a confirmation, no loot and no XP (for a rat that is too strong while the map is at its monster cap). Refused for the monster being fought (`dismissMonster`).
- **Discard item**: destroys one item or the whole stack after a confirmation, pays no gold (`discardItem`, refused during a fight).
- **Save game / Export**: *Save game* writes the save at once (`saveNow`) and shows a toast; autosave (2 s after a change, and on close/hide of the tab) is unchanged. *Export game save* copies the same `IDLZ2.` text as Import / backup to the clipboard.
- **Admin panel** (development builds only; resources, *Loot: chest*, *Heal* and the map tools): the cheat buttons in the game's own frame, folded into an icon with *Hide*; the choice is remembered (`idlarz.ui.*` in localStorage).
- **Map decor and water**: plains carry tiny flowers and grass tufts, swamps fallen dead trees (a second hash pass, purely visual), open lake water also glitters (a soft drifting-highlight strip over every open-water lake tile, `water_shimmer`), and open lake water plus deep swamp pools show small animated ripples (open water gets one to three per tile) (Settings > Life on the map turns the ripples and the critters off; reduced motion hides the ripples).
- **Ambient life**: every 14–30 s (the first after 6 s, at most 3 at once) a critter wanders over an owned tile in view for 6–10 s, chosen by biome: butterflies and bees on plains, forest, lake and coast, ladybugs on swamp, rabbits on hills and tundra, plus decoration-only **fish** (lakes, only on tiles with water on all four sides), **frogs** (swamp), **crabs** (coast), **goats** (hills) and **small wolves** (forest). A tile with a building uses its own biome. They are decoration only: they take no clicks and give nothing. Off in Settings, with a hidden tab, or with reduced-motion.

## 15. Monsters and combat

- **Spawning** (the server's, [11](11-offline-and-sealed-rolls.md)): the first rat appears 2 minutes after the land has a tile for it, then one every 15 minutes (chests: 10). A monster stands on **land of its allowlist, 2 to 9 tiles from the castle** (Chebyshev: never on the castle's neighbours, never on a lake or the coast, never beyond 9), with no building, chest or other monster on the tile and, from castle level 7, outside the castle's ward; the server chooses the tile (`lib/offline/placement.ts`, rules in `MonsterDef.spawn`), so a new game starts seeing rats once a tile two steps out is owned. There is no fixed cap: **monsters and chests together never fill more than 30 % of the free tiles** (`lib/spawnLimits.ts`, `SPAWN_LIMIT`), and monsters stop coming when every tile that suits them is taken. **Chests** are still placed by the browser and may appear on a lake (drawn as golden rings).
- **Levels**: every monster has a **level** (no tiers). A new rat appears at a level inside a window that rises with the castle level (castle 1: 1-3, castle 8 and up: 8-10, never above 10) and **gains +1 level every 30 minutes on the map, at most +10 above the level it appeared at** (appeared at 4: up to 14; at 8: up to 18). The growth limit and the interval are config values (`LIMITS.monsterGrowthLevels` / `monsterGrowthIntervalSec` in `config/limits.ts`, read through `limitOf`) so upgrades and the rebirth system can raise them later; each monster saves the level it appeared at. HP, attack, armor, reward and the item level of its drops all follow its level. The monster you are fighting does not age.
- **Bosses**: a monster of grade `boss` has its own spawn timer (3 hours of spawn time, one at a time; a rat never turns into one), a skull on its map badge and in the fight window (a BOSS banner with its crit chance, Stun resistance and armor), crits, resists Stun and carries boss-only drops (`lootSourceId`, the `boss` loot profile). The **Rat King** is the first: level 15-18 at castle 1 up to 22-25 at castle 10, growing up to +10 (level 35 at most). Armor reduces the damage of every click like a player's armor, so his HP is set so that his effective HP at level 15 is the old 15600.
- **Fighting is the server's; you start it.** **Click a monster** (or right-click it, *Fight*) and the server opens the fight against it, whatever your HP and with Auto-battle on or off (`CombatCommand` kind `fight`); from then on nobody clicks to hit. With **Auto-battle** on (**locked until your first rebirth** - `COMBAT.auto.minRebirths`: the checkbox and the menu entry are greyed out before it and the server ignores the switch; off by default, new accounts and rebirths) the server also sends you against the next monster, one at a time - the weakest waiting one first - as soon as your HP is back at 80 %: it clears the map for you, it is not needed to fight. Your weapon swings at its **attack speed** (attacks per second, `attacksPerSecond`: the weapon's speed, 1 bare-handed, +0.25 % per Dexterity point above 5) for your gear's damage times the attribute bonus of the weapon's type (Strength with a physical weapon, Intelligence with a staff or wand, §11); the monster swings at its own `attackSpeed` (rat 0.5/s, Rat King 1/3 s). A swing can **crit** (x2) with your Crit chance and a crit can become a **super crit** (x5) with 5 % plus your gear's Super Crit. Armor, dodge, life leech, stun and the potion cooldown work as before; the engine ticks every 0.1 s on the server and is fast-forwarded by every request.
- **HP**: about 100 at the start (`85 + vitality HP + gear`). It regenerates about 1 HP/s while no fight is active; the gear stat **HP Regen** adds to that and also works during fights. **Minor Healing Potions** (35 HP) and **Greater Healing Potions** (90 HP) share an 8 s cooldown in a fight (hotkeys 1-3 for the first three potion stacks) and work in and out of fights.
- **Mana and skills**: mana is `20 + 4 × Intelligence` plus gear (a fresh character has 40) and refills `0.5 + 0.05 × Intelligence` per second plus gear, in and out of fights; the current mana is saved. Two skills (`catalog/skills.ts`, hotkeys 4 and 5, buttons under the potions, the same two in the Equipment tab's ability slots): **Heal** (level 5, 20 mana, 12 s cooldown) restores 25 % of max HP, more with Intelligence (the same 2.4 % per point as magic weapons) and Spell Power, in and out of fights; **Stun** (level 10, 15 mana, 30 s cooldown) freezes the monster's attack timer for 10 s (fights only; the King's time limit keeps running; bosses can resist it). Skills do no damage: they buy time for the clicks. Against the King they change little (it is a race against his clock, so damage decides), and the starter kit never beats him ([generated/fight-sim.md](generated/fight-sim.md)). Cooldowns are not saved.
- **Losing**: at 0 HP you are knocked out (30 % HP back) and nothing is lost; the monster is at full HP again, is left alone for 5 minutes (a boss 10), and auto-battle waits until you have rested up to 80 % HP, then tries the next one. Switching Auto-battle off calls an open fight off the same way. Your HP, mana and cooldowns are the server's and survive a reload; a real absence (over 15 minutes) heals you.
- **Winning**: the combat window stays open with the **result**: the monster lying dead (grey, with a red cross) and what it left - gold, XP, hide and the drops (hover an item for its card), auto-sold gear, gold for drops that did not fit, your HP. It closes with its x, after 15 s (`COMBAT.victoryToastSec`, hover keeps it), or when another fight opens; a minimized window shows an orange dot on its icon. Gold and XP are a number of seconds of your income, plus a chance of gear and a potion (the loot tables in [10](10-loot-system.md)). A defeat or a timeout is shown in the same window.
- **Rat King** (the first boss; more monsters will follow, so nothing is balanced around him alone): 1000 HP at level 15 behind 15 armor and a 75-second clock, hits for 11 every 3 s with an 8 % crit (x1.5), resists Stun 20 %; higher levels follow the item level curve. He is fought last, once no other monster is ready, and on a timeout or a knockout he taunts you, stays on the map and is tried again after 10 minutes. With automatic swings he needs gear: a starter kit or plain wood never wins, full steel does not, full obsidian wins with seconds to spare, the Rattatui set comfortably (`docs/generated/fight-sim.md`, pinned by `tests/fightBalance.test.ts`).
- **Combat panel** (bottom right, above the minimap): the monster and its HP, your HP and mana, the potion and skill slots with their hotkeys (1-3 potions, 4 Heal, 5 Stun: **keys only, the slots are not buttons**), and the **Auto-battle** and **Auto-use** switches (Auto-use drinks below 40 % HP, casts Heal below 50 % and Stun when ready: a fixed preset). The window (and its icon) is shown only while a fight is open or its result is, and shrinks to an icon. **A click on a monster opens it at once** (maximized, with a loading state until the server has opened the fight). The HP and mana bars run smoothly between the server's answers (they move from the old number to the new one over the time since the last answer), the damage numbers follow the server's events, and the window shakes with a red flash when you are hurt (reduced-motion: flash only; there is no sound for a bite).

## 16. Items and the Equipment tab

The **Equipment tab** has no hero scene (that is the Character tab): a compact bar with the level orb and the XP bar on top (hover for the time to the next level), the **HP and MP bars** directly under it (half the width each, with their numbers), a small **ilvl** badge (the average item level of all worn pieces, empty slots left out; the merchant sells nothing more than 5 above it), the 13 slots in groups (Armor, Jewelry, Hands, and the **Skills** Heal and Stun beside the Hands), the Damage / Armor / Max HP / Crit / Regen readouts, and the bag beside it.

**Notices** all use one stack at the top centre of the screen, under the header (`.notice-stack` in `GameApp`): chest rewards and the short messages of the shared toast (`showToast`: saves, bag messages, every merchant sale, purchase and refresh, a full bag). Nothing is shown inside a window, so a message never moves the buttons under the cursor.

**Number notation**: every number in the UI goes through `utils/formatters.ts`: `formatNumber` (resources, prices, gold), `formatCompact` (stats and counts: plain up to 9,999 with at most one decimal, then 12.34K / M / B / T), `formatPercent` / `formatPct` (percentages, short from 5,000 %), `formatRate`, and the catalog's own stat texts (`formatStat` and friends in `catalog/stats.ts`). The one exception is the exact amount in a resource's header tooltip.

- **Slots**: head, shoulder, chest, hands, legs, feet (left), neck, cape, ring, ring, charm (right), weapon and shield (bottom), plus two ability slots (Heal and Stun; locked until their level). A 36-slot bag holds everything else; **Organize** (a button on the bag wherever it is shown, and an entry in the global right-click menu) merges potion stacks and sorts the gear by kind, slot, rarity, item level and quality, best first, and is refused during a fight.
- **Stats** (`catalog/stats.ts`): items give Damage (weapons only), Armor, Max HP, Crit Chance (%), Super Crit (%), HP Regen (/s), Luck, **Gold Find** (% more kill and chest gold), **Dodge** (% chance a monster hit misses, capped at 40 %) and the magic stats **Max Mana**, **Mana Regen**, **Spell Power** (% more healing) and **Life Leech** (% of click damage returned as HP, capped at 15 %). The staves, wands and tomes and the cloth gear carry the mana stats; staves and wands also scale with Intelligence. Every item has an **item level** (10 for all items for now; stats will scale with it, `statScale`) and the catalog keeps a base value per stat and tier (`STAT_BASE_BY_TIER`, rising with the tier). **Weapons** also have an **attack speed** (attacks per second, by weapon type: dagger 1.6 ... maul 0.6) and so a DPS; click combat does not use it yet, it is the base for attack-speed combat. Weapon damage is 3x its old value (starter sword 6) so rats die faster by click; the Rat King has 3x HP to stay as hard. Damage, Armor, Max HP, Crit and Regen are shown under the slots on the Equipment tab; the Armor tooltip shows the damage reduction.
- **Starter kit**: Wooden Sword (damage 2), Wooden Shield (armor 2) and 2 potions. Old saves get it on load.
- **Rarities** (`catalog/rarity.ts`): common (gray), uncommon (green), rare (blue), epic (violet), legendary (orange). Higher rarities glow stronger in the slots; epic and legendary pulse softly (reduced-motion: no pulse).
- **Gear families** (`catalog/items/*.ts`, all 12 slot types each: head, shoulder, chest, hands, legs, feet, neck, cape, ring, charm, off hand; weapons are separate). Each has a set bonus (3 / 6 / 12 pieces worn; both rings count):
  - **Wood and leather** (common, the starter kit and the commonest drop): leather cap, pauldrons, vest, gloves, leggings, boots, wooden pendant, leather cloak, wooden ring, acorn charm, wooden shield.
  - **Apprentice cloth** (common): little armor, lots of HP, a bit of regen, luck and crit; hat, mantle, robe, gloves, trousers, slippers, amulet, cape, ring, charm, tome.
  - **Knight's steel** (uncommon): honest armor; helm, pauldrons, cuirass, gauntlets, greaves, sabatons, gorget, cloak, ring, charm, steel shield. Helm, cuirass, gauntlets, greaves, sabatons, shield and the Iron Sword are sold by the merchant.
  - **Obsidian** (rare, never sold; old rats and good chests): the fancy tier with crit stats and spikes.
  - **Rattatui** (epic, **only from the Rat King**): the best armor and HP in the game plus crit, regen and luck; 3 pieces +3 Armor, 6 pieces +1 Damage, +4 % Crit, +15 Max HP, all 13 slots +1 Damage, +6 % Super Crit, +1 HP/s, +25 Max HP.
- **Weapons** (main hand, by type; crit and super crit values are the point of the bows and daggers): sword, mace, maul, axe, spear, dagger, bow, crossbow, staff, wand in wood/cloth, steel and obsidian grades, the Rattatui Fang Blade, and three legendary weapons (Emberbrand, Starfall Bow, Titan Maul). Full numbers: [generated/item-catalog.md](generated/item-catalog.md).
- **Potions**: Minor (35 HP, common, 80 gold) and Greater (90 HP, uncommon, 360 gold).
- **Tooltip**: an action-RPG style card attached to the edge of the item box (it does not follow the cursor and flips to the other side near the screen edge): name, rarity and slot, item level, the headline stat (weapon damage with attacks and damage per second, or armor), the other stats as a bullet list, set progress, flavour text and sell value. From the bag the worn piece is shown beside it and every number carries its change in green / red; stats only the worn piece has are listed as lost.
- **Gems**: the premium currency, its own balance (`state.gems`, not part of `resources`): every player starts with 1 000, saves from before it get 1 000 too, a rebirth keeps it. Nothing spends it yet (upgrades come later).
- **Controls**: hover for a tooltip; double-click a bag item to equip it (or drink a potion), right-click it for Equip / Drink, Sell one, Sell all (potion stacks) and Discard; click an equipped item to take it off; drag to rearrange the bag, to equip, or to unequip. Gear cannot be changed during a fight; taking off gear needs a free bag slot.
