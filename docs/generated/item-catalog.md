## Sets

Every threshold reached applies, so the bonuses add up; both rings count. The wood, cloth and steel families are plain loot (no set bonus), and so are the three legendary weapons and the potions: they are left out of this file until they get a role.

Set-only stats (no item rolls them) and where they pay out. The server derives every economy one from the saved gear (`lib/gearEconomy.ts`, `cloudSave/summary.ts`), so they count on the server's side as well:

| Stat | Effect | Applied by |
|---|---|---|
| Income | more of every resource but XP | income rates (`calculateResourceBreakdown`), so passive income, away income, kill gold and chest gold follow |
| XP Gain | more XP from every source | `xpGainPct`: tiles, passive XP, kills, chests and away time |
| Kill Rewards | more gold and XP from a kill | server kill payout (`killIncome`, `derived.killRewardPct`) |
| Away Income / Away Time | share of the income paid while away / hours paid | `offlineEfficiency` / `offlineCapHours` limits (`LimitContext.gear`) |
| Click Gold | more gold per map click | `clickForGold` (clicks are the browser's income, checked only by plausibility) |
| Damage / Max HP | percent on top of the flat stat | `damagePerHit` / `maxHp`, shared by the browser and the server's fights |
