The `human*` profiles play by the server's away rules (half income for at most 8 h of a gap, no purchases while away); `-builder2`, `-gems`, `-economy` and `-full` add the Gem shop's Builder #2, the Ember shop plans and the merchant's gold upgrades; `-rocks` and `-full` mine the rocks.
