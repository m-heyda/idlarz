# Kill sim

Generated by `npm run kill-sim` (part of `npm run docs:gen`). Do not edit by hand.

What the monsters pay against the income of a run (docs/plan/20-monster-families-and-rewards.md section 8). Per stage: castle level, hero level, gear tier and income per second come from the pacing sim (`npm run sim`, first run). Every monster is taken at the middle of its spawn window at that castle level; the hero wears the gear tier at the monster's item level and spends its points 60 % Strength, 40 % Vitality. A kill is priced with the real `killIncome` (cap, level multiplier; Luck, Gold Find and Kill Rewards % at 0). "Seconds" is the gold of one kill in seconds of the gold income. "Spawn share" is the gold a spawn of this type adds per spawn interval (900 s; bosses per 10800 s), as a share of the income of that interval, counting a monster only as often as the server spawns it (weighted by win chance, IDL-215) and as often as the hero wins. Target: all kills together about 15 % of the income; the weakest monster under about 1 % of a 10-minute income by the middle of the run.
