# bushes_map_details_mostly (164 files)

Low plants: Autumn bush, bushes with blue, orange, pink and red flowers, simple bushes 1 and 2, Cactus 1 and 2, Fern 1 and 2, Snow bush, Broken tree and Burned tree stumps, small shrubs. Sizes 16 to 432 px (the sheets are the large files). Same layout as the trees: `Assets`, `Assets_shadow`, `Assets_texture_shadow`, `Assets_texture_shadow_dark` (40 each) plus `*_source.png` sheets.

**Use for**

- Plains and forest ground clutter (simple bushes, ferns, flower bushes), swamp (ferns, red plants), tundra (Snow bush), hills (Cactus, Autumn bush), Blight (Burned tree).
- Berry and herb nodes for herbalist-style crafting ingredients (flower bushes read as "gatherable").
- Cheap variety on a big map: scatter by a seeded hash on empty tiles of a biome, so it costs no saved state.
