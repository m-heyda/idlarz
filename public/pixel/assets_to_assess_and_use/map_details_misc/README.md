# map_details_misc (7 sheets)

Sheets, not single sprites:

- `Exterior_objects.png` (352x656): a ruined temple (front and back, overgrown and brick versions), broken walls, columns, gravestones, three gargoyle statues in two sets, trees and shrubs.
- `Objects_interior.png` (384x256): a large winged gargoyle, red banners, red-cloth beds or altars, clay vases and pots, bone and skull piles, rubble, ember piles, a seated NPC and red chests.
- `Ghost.png` (480x512): a ghost monster, a 19-frame appear, idle and dissolve animation.
- `Leader_summon.png` (160x144): a red-hooded cultist, 13 frames of a summon animation.
- `Spikes.png`: floor spike trap animation frames.
- `Spots.png`: dirt and blood spot decals in several sizes.
- `Trees_grass_alternative_fit.png`: trees on a grass-fit alternative.

**Use for**

- Blight and Battlefield: Ghost and Leader_summon as monster or boss art (existing monsters are cut from sheets the same way, see `scripts/pixel` and `docs/plan/monster-art-prompts.md`), Spikes as trap decor, Spots as ground decals.
- Interior objects for a dungeon, camp or shrine scene; gravestones for a Battlefield graveyard landmark.
- Needs slicing before use (`scripts/pixel/extract_sheet.py`).
