Fantasy trees and mushrooms: beige-green and blue-green mushroom trees, Chanterelles (orange), Curved tree, Ent man and Ent woman, Light balls tree, Living gazebo (arched trees), Luminous tree, Mega tree (large, with a blue crystal), Swirling tree, Tree idols (deer, dragon, human, wolf), White-red mushrooms, White (dead) tree, Willow. `Assets` (soft grass shadow), `Assets_no_shadow` and `Assets_source.png`.

**Use for**

- Jungle (rebirth 1, IDL-106) and Blossom Orchard (rebirth 3, IDL-108): the four Tree idols (deer, dragon, human, wolf) are ready-made art for the biome **Idol buildings** of `docs/plan/18-biome-buildings-and-material-tiers.md`; the Mega tree and Living gazebo fit a landmark or a Producer.
- Ents as a monster or a boss (the art is a map prop, so a monster needs animation work).
- Mushroom trees for swamp and Blight variety.
