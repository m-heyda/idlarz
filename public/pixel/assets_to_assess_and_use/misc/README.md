# misc (102 files)

Dark-fantasy and cave props, each with a light and a dark shadow variant: demon skull, dinosaur skeleton (head, parts, full), human skeleton, caveman statue, bonfire, centipede, cocoon with web, Dark totems (4), magic circle (2 sizes, pentagram look), gates (a bone gate and a blue-crystal gate), mushrooms, beige rocks, crystals (blue-green, white, vertical ones) and two lair buildings (a blue crystal shrine, a spider lair). `Objects_separately/{16,32,64,128,256}` hold the singles grouped by size, `Cave_objects_source.png` is the sheet.

**Use for**

- Blight and Battlefield biomes (R9, IDL-110): lairs, bone fields, totems, pentagram circles, bonfires for camps.
- Boss spawn sites and danger decor in the outer distance bands (frontier, wilds; `docs/plan/20-monster-families-and-rewards.md` section 8).
- The crystal props can double as resource nodes (see the minerals folder).
