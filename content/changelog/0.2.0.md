# 0.2.0 - New biomes, bigger bags and a guide
date: 2026-11-10

- **Biomes unlock with every rebirth:** Jungle, Fields, Blossom Orchard, Blight and Battlefield open one per rebirth, and their tiles produce more than the basic ones. A short screen tells you what you just unlocked.
- **Bosses keep their own loot:** each biome boss has its own drops, so you know which boss to hunt for which gear.
- **Bigger bags:** the bag starts at 60 slots, Pack Rat gives more per level, and the new Ember upgrade Deep Pockets adds permanent space.
- **Class passives:** each class now has an income bonus and a combat bonus, shown in the Character window.
- **Meet Brother Alder:** a guide who walks you through your first steps, points at what to click and explains new resources, income and rebirth as you reach them. Every lesson is kept in a Guide journal, you can skip it at any time and turn it off in Settings.
- **Changelog:** this window now has one folded row per version.
