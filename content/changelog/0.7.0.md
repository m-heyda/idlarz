# 0.7.0 - The Ember shop, builders and caps
date: 2026-11-17

- **The Ember shop is bigger and stronger.** Every gold upgrade at the merchant now has a much stronger permanent twin in the Ember shop (selling, Luck, income, meat and hide, click power, all seven fighting upgrades). Ancestral Tithe has an **Eternal Tithe** twin that adds +50% Embers per level.
- **Your Embers come back.** Because the shop changed, every Ember you earned is unspent again. Spend them in the new shop. Your income bonus from Embers stays exactly as it was, and you keep auto-potion for free if you had it.
- **New Ember upgrades:** raise the castle cap from 10 to **15** (Castle Expansion), raise how high buildings and Idols can go (Master Masonry, Idol Mastery), raise the tile discount cap, shorter build times for the castle, buildings and Idols, a bigger starting income for every new run (**Starting Treasury**), more starting supplies, an extra builder, and the one-time **Eternal Vigil** (full income and up to 72 hours while you are away) and **Imperial Decree** (the castle bonus counts double).
- **Auto-potion is now bought** in the Ember shop (Potion Reflex). Everyone who had already rebirthed keeps it.
- **Castle level 15:** a new achievement, **Grand Architect**, for reaching it.
- **Click Power** now has five levels and ends stronger than before, but each step is smaller. **Click Mastery** in the Ember shop is stronger.
- **Pack Rat** gives +4 bag slots per level (what you already bought keeps its old +8 per level), and **Deep Pockets** gives +8 slots per level with five levels.
- **A second builder:** buy it in the gem shop for 1,000 gems and build two things at once. The Ember shop's Extra Builder adds a third, the most there is.
- **A building that is being upgraded** shows an arrow next to its level, like **3↑**.
- **Idols take time to build now:** 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, 12 hours and 24 hours for levels 1 to 5, and the extract they need from level 3 costs five times more. An Idol you already learned still comes back at once after a rebirth.
- **More stone:** tundra and plains now produce a little stone, so stone is less of a bottleneck.
- **Playing as a guest** got a few small improvements to the landing page and the first steps.
- **The Bestiary is open.** Every monster has an entry: art, family, traits, a line of lore (some of it funny), where and when it appears, your kills, deaths and best time. A monster you have not fought yet is a dark "?" until the first fight. Open it from the dock; the window can be resized by dragging its corner and re-flows to fit.
- **Mastery with real bonuses.** Each monster has four tiers (10, 100, 500 and 2,500 kills; bosses 1, 5, 25 and 100). Press **Claim** on a reached tier for a permanent bonus **against that monster**: more damage, less damage taken, more gold, more XP or a better chance of gear drops. The top tier also pays gems. **Claim all** takes everything waiting, and the dock icon shows how many rewards are ready.
- **Stats and drops at any level.** Tier 1 shows a monster's stats and a +/- control to see how they grow with its level; the 100-kill tier shows its full drop table, scaled to the level you are looking at.
- **Biome goals:** when every monster of a biome, its boss included, reaches the same tier, claim a collection bonus that works against all monsters of that biome (the last tier pays gems).
- **21 new monsters:** every biome now has five monsters and a boss, including glass-cannon, armored and brute types. They wear a grey stand-in picture until their art is ready.
- **New monster drops:** monsters now leave ingredients such as bacon, blood, horns, feathers, clay, ore, glowberries, moonstone and gems, matched to what they are and growing with their level. The merchant buys them today; crafting will use them later.
- **New achievements:** **Scholar IV** (50 filled entries), **Archivist** (claim 10, 50 and 150 mastery tiers, and **Complete Record** for every tier of every monster, with a gem reward) and **Field Naturalist** (claim biome goals, up to all 48).
