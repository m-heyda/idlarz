# 0.9.8 - Paragon milestones
date: 2026-11-29

- **A new deed, Paragon, follows your paragon level.** Tiers at paragon 1, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 750 and 1,000 pay gems and deed points when you claim them.
- **Milestones from paragon 100 up give a title and one bonus paragon point.** Claim a milestone tier in the Hall of Deeds to get the point to spend in the Paragon tab and a new title to wear: Paragon, Exalted, Ascendant, Eternal, Immortal, Demigod and Celestial.
- **Your paragon mark wears a frame.** In the Rankings and in the Paragon window the mark beside your name gets a coloured frame as you climb: bronze at 100, silver 200, gold 300, ruby 400, emerald 500, amethyst 750 and diamond at 1,000.
- Requests to the server are now limited per account (far above normal play, mining rocks included).
