# 0.9.8 - Paragon milestones
date: 2026-11-29

- **A new deed, Paragon, follows your paragon level.** Tiers at paragon 1, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 750 and 1,000 pay gems and deed points when you claim them.
- **Milestones from paragon 100 up give a title and one bonus paragon point.** Claim a milestone tier in the Hall of Deeds to get the point to spend in the Paragon tab and a new title to wear: Paragon, Exalted, Ascendant, Eternal, Immortal, Demigod and Celestial.
- **Your paragon mark wears a frame.** In the Rankings and in the Paragon window the mark beside your name gets a coloured frame as you climb: bronze at 100, silver 200, gold 300, ruby 400, emerald 500, amethyst 750 and diamond at 1,000.
- Requests to the server are now limited per account (far above normal play, mining rocks included).
- **Monsters are stronger after each rebirth.** Every rebirth lifts the levels monsters spawn at by 4, so a hero who has rebirthed several times no longer meets level 20 monsters at castle level 5.
- **Monsters spawn much farther from your castle**, up to 40 tiles out, so the jungle, fields, orchard, blight and battlefield monsters finally show up on your land. The farther out, the higher the level.
- **Bosses come twice as fast:** one every 1.5 hours instead of 3, and one every hour from your third rebirth.
- **New Ember shop upgrade, Wild Growth:** monsters grow a level faster, from every 15 minutes down to every 5 minutes at level 10, so they reach their top level (and pay more) sooner.
- **More to do late in the game:** the map can now hold more monsters and chests at once after rebirths (40 in a first run, 10 more per rebirth, up to 100).
- **New Ember shop upgrade, Battle Queue:** each of its 5 levels adds 2 places to the battle queue (up to 20).
- **High-level monsters are easier.** Normal monsters above level 20 now gain health and damage much more slowly per level, so the tundra and later regions (after the rebirth level lift) are beatable again. Monsters already on your map and a fight in progress follow the new numbers at once.
- **The Changelog is now in the game.** A new button in the top bar, next to the Kingdom overview, opens the release notes in a window, so you can read what is new without leaving your realm.
