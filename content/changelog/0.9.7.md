# 0.9.7 - Simpler crafting
date: 2026-11-28

- **Gear is built from planks, leather and ingots, not from thousands of raw wood and stone.** The Starter gear (grade 1) still takes plain resources. From the Hide grade up, weapons, shields and jewelry take **Plank Bundles** and armor takes **Cured Leather**, plus the ingots, nails and gems they already needed.
- **Bigger sizes for the better grades.** A Plank Crate is ten bundles and a Plank Pallet twenty crates (the same for Leather Stack and Leather Bale), crafted in seconds, so a piece asks for 6 to a few hundred of one size instead of a hundred thousand raw wood. The Plank Bundle and Cured Leather themselves are bigger now (2,000 wood and 1,000 stone, 800 hide and 500 coal), and a Cloth Bolt takes 1,000 wool. The total cost of a piece is the same as before.
- **Every monster ingredient has a use now.** Hollow Log goes into the Stormstring and the Starlight Wand. Dark Horn and Dark Shard are ground into the new **Dark Powder**, which every Distilled Ichor needs (and the Dawnsplitter). Dark Potion goes into the Elixirs of Warding and Fortitude, and a Fire Drop into every Obsidian, Runed and Ancient ring and amulet.
- **Leather Satchel** is worth 2,800 gold now and takes one Cured Leather and ten minutes to make.
