# 0.9.2 - Epic finds
date: 2026-11-24

- **Epic (purple) items drop much earlier.** Until now an epic could only drop from item level 55, so most players never saw one. Iron, Steel and Knight pieces (item levels 19 to 54) can now drop as epic, Iron and Steel pieces also as rare, and Hide pieces as rare. Normal monsters roll epic a little more often, and a long streak of kills without one makes the next epic likelier (it starts over when one drops).
- **Rarer items are stronger.** A rare piece has 10 % more damage, armor and health than an uncommon one of the same level, an epic 25 % more and a legendary 50 % more. Every affix on them is worth the same bonus on top of its roll, so an epic Sharp or of Power is clearly better than a rare one.
- **Named legendary weapons and crafted gear.** The named legendary weapons keep their numbers. Crafting still rolls the same rarities as before; a crafted rare or epic piece gets the same stat bonus as a dropped one.
- **An epic or legendary drop gets its own notice.** When a fight leaves an epic or better piece in your bag, a purple (epic) or orange (legendary) notice with its name stays at the top of the screen for a few seconds, also when the fight window is minimised.
- **Legendary weapons with perks.** Eight more legendary weapons can drop, each with a fixed bonus that adds to your gear like a set bonus: **Dawnbringer Trident** (+25 % income, from the Jungle boss), **Reaper's Crescent** (+25 % kill rewards, from the Fields boss), **Frostbloom Fan** (+15 % XP), **Voidrune Blade** (+15 away income), **Nightfall Edge** (+15 % click gold), **Stormtrident** (+15 % max HP), **Cinderflight Bow** (+15 Gold Find) and **Cindercrescent Glaive** (+10 % damage). The last six come from chests and, very rarely, from monsters of level 15 and up. Their bonuses count towards the same caps as set bonuses.
