# 0.9.2 - Epic finds
date: 2026-11-24

- **Epic (purple) items drop much earlier.** Until now an epic could only drop from item level 55, so most players never saw one. Iron, Steel and Knight pieces (item levels 19 to 54) can now drop as epic, Iron and Steel pieces also as rare, and Hide pieces as rare. Normal monsters roll epic a little more often, and a long streak of kills without one makes the next epic likelier (it starts over when one drops).
- **Rarer items are stronger.** A rare piece has 10 % more damage, armor and health than an uncommon one of the same level, an epic 25 % more and a legendary 50 % more. Every affix on them is worth the same bonus on top of its roll, so an epic Sharp or of Power is clearly better than a rare one.
- **Named legendary weapons and crafted gear.** The named legendary weapons keep their numbers. Crafting still rolls the same rarities as before; a crafted rare or epic piece gets the same stat bonus as a dropped one.
- **An epic or legendary drop gets its own notice.** When a fight leaves an epic or better piece in your bag, a purple (epic) or orange (legendary) notice with its name stays at the top of the screen for a few seconds, also when the fight window is minimised.
- **Legendary weapons with perks.** Eight more legendary weapons can drop, each with a fixed bonus that adds to your gear like a set bonus: **Dawnbringer Trident** (+25 % income, from the Jungle boss), **Reaper's Crescent** (+25 % kill rewards, from the Fields boss), **Frostbloom Fan** (+15 % XP), **Voidrune Blade** (+15 away income), **Nightfall Edge** (+15 % click gold), **Stormtrident** (+15 % max HP), **Cinderflight Bow** (+15 Gold Find) and **Cindercrescent Glaive** (+10 % damage). The last six come from chests and, very rarely, from monsters of level 15 and up. Their bonuses count towards the same caps as set bonuses.
- **Three more legendary weapons, and two with special effects.** **Runeforged Titanhammer** (-25 % on every castle, building and Idol timer, from the Blight boss), **Tidecaller Axe** (+15 % chest speed), **Cindercrescent Glaive** (+10 % damage and **Burn**: a critical hit sets the monster alight for 30 % of that hit per second for 3 seconds) and **Amethyst Spire** (**Potion refund**: 15 % of the potions you drink in a fight are not used up). The timer cut shares the 60 % cap with the Ember upgrades and the Dwarf Statue.
- **Two Gem shop items are cheaper.** **Name colours** and **Overnight Orders** now cost 750 gems instead of 1000, so buying one no longer takes your whole starting purse. Nothing changes for anyone who already owns them.
- **Four new gear stats.** Gear can now roll **Crit Damage** (rings and necklaces; up to +50 % on your crit multipliers), **Armor Penetration** (weapons; up to 30 % of a monster's armor ignored), **Thorns** (off hands; up to 25 % of a monster's hit comes back on it) and **Heal on Kill** (rings and charms; up to 8 % of your max HP restored when a monster dies). Armor Penetration also counts in the win chance. The Character window lists the three that apply to fights when something you wear gives them.
- **Four economy stats on gear.** From Steel grade (G4) up, hands and chest carry **Build Speed** (shorter castle, building and Idol timers, up to 15 % from gear), hands also carry **Farm Yield** (more of every farm product, up to 25 %, in the same bucket as the Harvest Scarecrow), cape and necklace carry **Tile Discount** (cheaper tiles, up to 10 points) and head and shoulders carry **Boss Timer** (more seconds on a boss fight's clock, up to 30 s). They count together with your other bonuses under the usual caps (60 % build time, the tile discount cap).
- **Legendary perks stand out.** A legendary's fixed bonus now has its own gold "Legendary perk" block in the item card instead of a line among the blue affixes.
- **Richer resource chests.** A chest holds a bundle of wood, stone, coal or food more often (about one in three instead of one in four) and the bundle is bigger: 20 to 45 minutes of each resource's income instead of 10 to 30. Gold chests are unchanged.
- **Rings can roll Attack Speed.** A new ring affix (Swift / of Haste) adds up to 25 % more attacks per second on top of your weapon and Dexterity.
- **Thorns count in the win chance.** The win chance shown for a monster now knows that your Thorns hurt it back, so it can be higher with a thorned off hand.
- **Item tier.** The item card's level line now starts with the tier of its item level: Tier I (1-19), II (20-39), III (40-59), IV (60-79) or V (80 and up).
- **Biome milestones.** Owning many tiles of one biome makes its Producer and Extractor stronger: +25 % at 75, 150 and 300 tiles of plains or forest, at 100, 200 and 400 of swamp or tundra, and at 125, 250 and 500 of each rebirth biome (hills, lake and coast have none). The milestones show as their own line in the income tooltip, and the building text and tooltip count them in the biome bonus.
- **Rebirth gives 20 attribute points.** Every rebirth adds a flat 20 attribute points to your pool for good, on top of the points your level earns, so a character that has been reborn five times has 100 more points to spend. They are free after the rebirth like the rest, and a respec gives them back too.
