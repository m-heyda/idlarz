# 0.8.7 - Merchant and Kingdom tweaks
date: 2026-11-18

- **Restock starts cheap again.** The price of the Merchant's **Restock** button no longer stays high for good. It goes back to its first price after a **rebirth** (and you get the free restocks back), and it also starts over **24 hours of play** after the first paid restock, so it rises only while you keep restocking.
- **Kingdom overview shows the real cap.** The buildings list now reads, for example, **Lv 1 / 5**: the highest level a building can reach with what you own (and **Master Masonry** or **Idol Mastery** from the Ember shop), not the level the castle allows right now. You can see at a glance how far each building can still go.
- **Merchant ledger in categories.** The Resources tab groups the stock ledger into **Base materials**, **Farm products** and **Extracts**. Click a category title to fold or open it; the Merchant remembers what you chose. Picking a resource from the top bar opens its category.
- **Fixes.** The Mire Matriarch's venom now cuts your regeneration only while you are hurt (full health no longer loses HP to it), and the auto-battle win estimate counts venom stacks building up over the fight and one shared potion cooldown. If the connection drops while you break a rock, your hits are kept and re-sent, so the loot of a break the server already paid is not lost. Rocks are no longer placed on tiles that hold a building, and the tile XP bonus from gear is no longer lost when you take the gear off right after buying land.
