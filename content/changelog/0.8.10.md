# 0.8.10 - More from the rocks
date: 2026-11-19

- **Rocks give more kinds of crafting materials.** Geodes now come out of Silver and Rich ore rocks, Obsidian and Dark Shards out of Crystal and Legendary rocks, and the Legendary rock can also hold an Emerald Star or, rarely, a Heart Gem.
- **Cracked Beast Claws and Void Amethyst Shards are crafting materials now.** They drop into the crafting bag instead of the bag and can no longer be sold. Stacks you already hold stay sellable.
- **Ember Coal is a tier 3 material** (it was tier 2), which is how its tooltip reads now.
- **New pictures for the healing potions and the Glass Vial.** The Healing Flask is now a round red potion, the Greater Healing Flask a conical flask, and the Glass Vial an empty round flask with a cork.
- **New farm upgrade: the Rooster.** Once every chicken slot of the pen is filled, you can turn chickens into roosters, one per level, up to 5 levels (5M food for the first, six times more for each next one). A rooster lays and feeds like **5 chickens**, and every rooster gives all your chickens **+10%** meat and eggs. Your roosters strut around the pen.
- **New crafting component: Arcane Essence.** Craft it from a Glass Vial, 3 Glowberries and 2 Dried Herbs (crafting level 3). It is the base of the magic potions to come, so nothing uses it yet.
- **Dawnsplitter, the legendary axe, has its own picture** (a flaming magma axe) and much stronger numbers on paper. It is still shown only in the Legendary list of the crafting window (level 99).
