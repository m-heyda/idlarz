# 0.8.12 - Three new secrets
date: 2026-11-19

- **Three new secret deeds in the Secrets list.** They are marked `???` until you find them, so no spoilers here beyond this: one for the keyboard, one for the clock and one for browsing every window in the dock.
- **Fixed accounts being paid from an old castle level after a long idle run.** If you left the game running for many hours (an overnight auto-clicker, say), the server could keep paying your income, chests and offline pay from your castle level at the time and ignore the levels you had really built. It now believes the levels once the real time that has passed covers their upgrade timers.
