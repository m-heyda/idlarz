# 0.6.0 - Keep your legendaries through a rebirth
date: 2026-11-17

- **Keep legendaries when you rebirth:** before you rebirth, a new window lists what you **lose** and what you **keep**. Then you choose which legendary pieces to carry into the next run. Rebirth number N lets you keep up to N pieces (1 on the first, 2 on the second, up to 15). Pieces from your bag or the ones you wear both count, and they arrive in your bag.
- **Pick with a [+] slot:** each free slot opens a small bag of your legendaries. Hover a piece to see it, click it to keep it, click it again to put it back. If you own legendaries but chose none, the game asks once more before you rebirth.
- **Stronger rebirths:** Embers now grow faster with the land you own, so every tile above the requirement is worth more than before, and the castle level still counts. The income bonus from Embers is also higher. A first rebirth at castle 6 with 400 tiles now pays **32 Embers** instead of 18.
- **Newer windows:** tabbed windows, a search box in Crafting, a **Unique** rarity look, and tidier rebirth and castle cards and tooltips.
