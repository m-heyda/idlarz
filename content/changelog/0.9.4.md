# 0.9.4 - Paragon
date: 2026-11-26

- **Levels past 100: paragon.** At level 100 your XP keeps counting: every further step is a paragon level, up to 5,000. The Hero window's XP bar turns into a paragon bar ("Paragon 34 · 97%") with the time to the next level, the Stats tab shows `Level 100 ✦ 34` and the next paragon level, and the Rankings mark players with their paragon level (✦). XP you earned past level 100 before this update counts too. The first paragon level takes about 0.7 billion XP and each one a little more; paragon 100 is around 77 billion and paragon 500 around 600 billion. The points to spend and the upgrades they buy come in the next updates.
