# 0.8.8 - Mining tweaks
date: 2026-11-18

- **Rocks and ores appear much less often.** A new rock now turns up about every **20 minutes** of play (it was every two and a half, and half as often as a chest), and the map holds far fewer at a time (at most 5, and a small share of your free land). Luck still makes them come a little sooner and makes the better kinds likelier.
- **Dropped ores keep their shape.** The pictures that fall out of a broken rock (Gold Ore and the other ores) were squeezed narrow when the amount under them was short. They now keep their own size and proportions.
- **New Ember upgrade: Mining Power (50 Embers, one-time).** Every click on a rock counts as **2 clicks**, so rocks break twice as fast. You find it in the Ember shop under **Economy**. The server counts it too, and the limit on how fast a person can click still applies to real clicks.
