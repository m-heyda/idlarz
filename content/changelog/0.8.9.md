# 0.8.9 - Idol upkeep
date: 2026-11-19

- **Idol upgrades past level 2 ask for much more Extractor material.** Levels 3, 4 and 5 now cost **10K, 100K and 500K** of the biome material (it was 10K, 20K and 40K), and every level above 5 (once the Ember shop lifts the cap) costs **5 times** the one before: 2.5M, 12.5M, 62.5M and so on.
- **Sign-in, password reset, guest and "A new chapter" screens use the new window frame**, the same stone frame and title bar as the rest of the game. Nothing on them works differently.
