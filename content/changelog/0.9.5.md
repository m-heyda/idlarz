# 0.9.5 - Pets and fixes
date: 2026-11-26

- **Pets tab.** The Pets tab in the Hero window now shows the companions that are coming: a cat, a hamster, a dachshund and a rabbit, greyed out for now. You will unlock them with achievements in a later update.
- **Rebirth: choose what to respec.** The rebirth confirmation has two tick boxes. "Take back my attribute points" is on as before; untick it to keep your attributes as they are and only add the 20 extra points of the rebirth. If you spent paragon points, a second box takes them back for free (they stay by default).
- **Tile discount.** The Treasury Charter now raises the tile discount cap by 3 points per level (75% at level 10, never above 80%) instead of 5. No combination of Charisma, Thrift, Bulk Discount, gear and paragon could reach the old 95%, so tiles stay a real cost late in a run.
