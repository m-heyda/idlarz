# 0.9.6 - Crafting rebalance
date: 2026-11-27

- **Reaching crafting level 60 is realistic now.** Crafting gives 40 times more crafting XP per craft, and the gear of the later grades (Steel and up) costs about a twentieth of the materials it did. A player who crafts every day gets to level 60 in about six weeks; levels 61 to 80 stay a long goal.
- **Smaller batches.** One request now queues at most 25 pieces of gear, potions or goods (a legendary is always one), and a full batch never takes longer than 12 hours. Materials such as planks, ingots and nails can be made 1,000 at a time and take a few seconds each, and the crafting stash holds up to 10 million of each.
- **Slower gear, faster materials.** Gear takes from 2 minutes (Starter) to 30 minutes (the top grades) per piece, a legendary 3 hours. A batch that was queued before this update still finishes as it was.
- **Crafted gear sells for little.** The merchant pays a twentieth of the normal price for a piece you crafted, so crafting stays about gear and levels and is no source of gold.
- **Distilled Ichor from level 35** (it was 60), so the enchant craft is open earlier.
- **Paragon: Life leech is +0.12 % per point** (at most +3 % with all 25 points, it was +0.2 % and +5 %). Combined with Damage and Maximum life it made a bad kit win boss fights it should lose; fights still get faster and safer with every combat point.
- **A new Paragon window.** Each category has its own colour (Combat red, Economy gold, Fortune green, Realm violet), every upgrade has an icon in a medallion with a ring that fills as you add points, and the upgrades with no cap are octagons with an infinity mark. The header shows your paragon level in a ring, a bar to the next level and your free points as a glowing gem.
