# 0.9.3 - Finding things and finishing touches
date: 2026-11-25

- **Find things from the Kingdom overview.** The Kingdom overview's last section, "Find on the map", is now a grid of equal tiles: Chests, Ore nodes, Monsters, Bosses, Strongest, Golden, Fully grown and Still growing, each with how many there are. Click a tile to ring just those on the map (the same as the buttons under the minimap, one kind at a time) and the tile lights up. It replaces "Monsters on the map".
- **Monster chests have their own pictures.** Iron, Mossy, Coral, Frost, Magma, Royal and Arcane chests no longer share the wooden chest's picture.
- **Attribute rules are spelled out.** Every attribute tooltip says that income, mana and crit chance count every point while damage, armor, attack speed and crit damage count the points above 5, the Stats tab shows the gold bonus your attributes give (a new hero starts at +25 %), and the tutorial says the same.
- **Set pieces.** A set's card now says the main hand is part of the set, so wearing another weapon loses a piece.
- **The same item card in the fight window.** Items in the victory loot and the potions on the fight bar show the full item card, like the Bag and the Merchant.
- **Fixed: the 20 attribute points of a rebirth could be refused.** Some accounts could not spend the last 20 points after a rebirth because the server counted one rebirth too few; it now counts them all.
- **Golden monsters sparkle.** A golden monster on the map now twinkles with two small stars, so it is easy to spot (they stand still if you have reduced motion on). The Bestiary's line about golden monsters also says they always drop an Arcane chest.
- **Epic and legendary items glow again.** The rim of an epic, legendary or unique piece breathes gently in the Bag, the Merchant and your gear slots, as it did before the new look (it stays still with reduced motion on).
- **A tidier Build here window.** The buildings of a tile are split into two groups you can fold: **Build new** (not built yet) and **Already owned** (with their level; a Buy button appears there only when you have a free slot for one more copy). Moving a building to another tile is gone: the Move button and its half-price offer no longer exist.
- **Fixed: legendary perks were listed twice.** A legendary's fixed perk showed in its stat group and again in its gold Legendary Perk block; it now shows only in the block.
