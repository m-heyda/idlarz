# 0.9.1 - Polish and quality of life
date: 2026-11-24

- **The Build here window has two columns and a fixed order.** Producer, Extractor, Idol, then the general buildings always stay in the same place, also when one of them is built elsewhere and offers "Move here".
- **General buildings explain themselves.** The Scriptorium, Watchtower and the others now show "Its bonus" (for example +1 % crafting XP now, +2 % at the next level) and how their copies add up, instead of a land bonus of +0 %.
- **Clearer Rebirth screen.** The button now reads "Rebirth & earn N Embers", the note explains that the permanent income bonus grows with every Ember you have ever earned (so more tiles and a higher castle raise both) and that the castle bonus is separate. Pack Rat now says its bag slots are kept through rebirth.
- **Hourglass Sand drops.** The Banner Wraith (rare) and the Cinder Warlord (always) drop Hourglass Sand, so the Elixir of Time Shift can be crafted. Venom, Dye and Royal Jelly now say honestly that nothing uses them yet.
- **The castle window saves your attention while it builds.** During an upgrade the cost you already paid is shown greyed as "Current upgrade, already paid", and the next upgrade is the main panel with its green and red costs, so you can see what to save for.
- **Small income rates are readable.** The merchant ledger shows income per second with two decimals (for example +0.38/s) and "<0.01/s" for tiny ones, instead of rounding farm products down to +0/s.
- **Elixir of Arcana.** A new elixir (crafting level 26: Arcane Essence, Glowberry, a Glass Vial and Herbs) that gives **+50 % mana regeneration for 3 minutes**, so the Stun and Heal spells come back sooner.
- **A calmer buff row.** With more than three potion effects running, the combat window folds them into one "Buffs (N)" line that opens on a click.
- **A fuller Kingdom overview.** Land now shows each biome's share of your tiles (for example 21.7 %). Buildings are grouped by biome, always in the order Producer, Extractor, Idol, then the general buildings, and a building of a biome you have not unlocked yet says "Locked: rebirth N" instead of "Not built". The count reads "built of available". "Monsters on the map" also counts golden monsters, fully grown and still growing monsters, unopened chests and ore nodes.
- **Compare a ring with either ring you wear.** Hovering a ring in the bag or at the merchant compares it with the first free ring slot; hold **Ctrl** to compare it with your other ring. The list of changes says which ring it replaces ("Difference: replacing Ring 2"). Double-click equips into the first free ring slot; **Ctrl + double-click** equips into the other one.
- **One place for the changes.** Item cards no longer put green or red brackets beside every stat or print a separate "Properties lost when equipped" list; the Difference section lists every change once, gains first and losses after.
- **Readable stat names.** Item cards and the Stats tab spell stats out (Crit Chance, HP Regen, Mana Regen, Spell Power, Attack Speed, Strength) instead of CRIT CH, HP REG, MP REG, SPELL PWR, ATK SPD and STR.
- **A compact Build here window.** Smaller art and cost chips, one line of text per building (the full line is on hover and in Details), and short buttons: "Buy" and "Move".
- **Tooltip hints no longer break in the middle.** A key hint such as "Dbl-click equip" stays on one line and long lists wrap between hints.
- **Number notation.** Settings has a new **Number notation** choice: suffixes (1.23M), scientific (1.23e6) or engineering (12.3e6). Suffixes now go on to Sp, Oc, No and Dc.
