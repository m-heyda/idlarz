# 0.5.0 - Rankings, a new landing page and a reworked combat window
date: 2026-11-17

- **Rankings:** a Rankings button in the bottom menu opens a board of players with podium trophies, bigger avatars and name effects. Only games on the current chapter are ranked.
- **A new landing page:** a new realm picture and logo, the changelog on parchment on the left and the account window on the right, set a little higher so they overlap the logo. The footer line is gone. A **Play as guest** button waits under the login window, switched off for now (guest games will not be ranked).
- **Welcome back, in sections:** the window is more compact, so more resources fit without scrolling, and its blocks fold. Your daily bonus can be claimed right there, and the separate daily window no longer pops up after it. If you already claimed today, the block is simply not shown.
- **Daily bonus, redesigned:** the 7-day board has four tiles to a row with a wide day 7 tile. Day 7 now pays gems **and a chest of materials** (a few kinds of resources, worth minutes of your own income).
- **Combat window, reworked:** the monster's HP bar sits on the stage above the monster, your HP and mana share one row, and potions and skills are one hotbar of small icons. Skills show their cost and cooldown in a tooltip, and the cooldown runs over the icon like a potion's. A won fight shows the monster in grey with a skull on it, and a smaller loot row.
- **Monsters have more stats:** the monster tooltip shows attack speed, damage per second, crits and stun resistance. The header reads **Lv 14/18 ↑** while it still grows, and a green **Lv 15** once it is fully grown.
- **Skills:** new Heal and Stun icons, a sound when you cast Heal or Stun, and a **Skills** tab in the Hero window (coming soon).
- **Sounds:** a coin sound when a buy or sell goes through (merchant, farm, Ember shop, gem shop), and a gulp when you drink a potion.
- **Crafting:** recipes are listed as Weapons, Armor and Other, each by the level it needs, with a level tag on what you cannot craft yet. Three legendary weapons are shown as a far-off goal. Trade goods are hidden for now, and Cured Leather and Plank Bundle have their own pictures.
- **Farm:** the stats box in the corner is gone, and the Upgrades button opens a full-window upgrade screen in the same style as the Merchant's.
- **Builder queue:** the construction plates in the corner are smaller, ready for more builders later.
- **Merchant and tooltips:** the long note under the stock ledger is gone, upgrades that are maxed show only their purple gems, and the treasure chest tooltip has its picture back.
- **Idols:** upgrade prices no longer drop after a rebirth, and Idol levels now go up to 100.
- **Look and feel:** a map that fades from grey to colour while your monsters and chests load, a better empty cape slot, and a new tick box.
- **Character picker:** the big portrait is smaller so the whole card fits.
