# 0.3.0 - Buildings, a new farm and new monsters
date: 2026-11-11

- **Buildings are here:** every home biome has a Producer that boosts its tiles and an Extractor that makes a new crafting material (Herbs, Sap, Iron Ore, Spores, Frostshard, Pearls, Salt). Buildings have five levels, share one builder with your castle, and keep upgrading and paying while you are away. The new **Build here** window shows each building's picture, what it makes and its price.
- **A reworked castle:** ten new looks as it grows and seven skins to choose from.
- **A bigger farm:** sheep replace the deer and give **wool**, the new Shearing Shed upgrade boosts it, and the farm window has a fresh meadow look and wider upgrade cards.
- **New monsters:** the hills, the coast, the lake and the jungle now have their own monsters and bosses.
- **Monster discovery:** monsters you have not met yet show up as a dark "?" until your first fight, and your kills of each one are counted.
- **Achievements and avatars:** twelve achievements pay out gems, and every medal has its own art. Pick an avatar from your class's portraits, and earn special ones through achievements.
- **Potion belt:** belts now give extra potion slots, faster potion cooldowns, stronger potions and a few more bag slots.
- **A cleaner look:** a simpler hero card (click your portrait to change your avatar), new stone menu icons, and faces for the people behind the merchant's counter.
- **Under the hood:** faster loading checks, refreshed pictures everywhere, and a few fixes.
