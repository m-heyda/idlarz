# 0.3.0 - Buildings, a new farm and new monsters
date: 2026-11-11

- **Buildings are here:** every home biome has a Producer that boosts its tiles and an Extractor that makes a new crafting material (Herbs, Sap, Iron Ore, Spores, Frostshard, Pearls, Salt). Buildings have five levels, share one builder with your castle, and keep upgrading and paying while you are away. The new **Build here** window shows each building's picture, what it makes and its price.
- **A new chapter:** everyone starts fresh. You keep your login and your character name, begin with **1,000 gems** and the **Early tester** avatar, and pick your name, class and avatar again. The tutorial walks you through the new game.
- **Idols:** every home biome has an Idol, a statue that gives a permanent bonus (for example more XP, Luck, damage or faster chests). You build it once, level it up to five times, and its level stays with you when you rebirth. Put it back on any tile of its biome for free.
- **A reworked castle:** ten new looks as it grows and seven skins to choose from.
- **A bigger farm:** sheep replace the deer and give **wool**, the new Shearing Shed upgrade boosts it, and the farm window has a fresh meadow look and wider upgrade cards.
- **New monsters:** the hills, the coast, the lake and the jungle now have their own monsters and bosses.
- **Monster discovery:** monsters you have not met yet show up as a dark "?" until your first fight, and your kills of each one are counted.
- **Achievements and avatars:** twelve achievements pay out gems, and every medal has its own art. Each class has its own two portraits (the other classes' faces are shown locked), and you earn special ones through achievements.
- **Potion belt:** belts now give extra potion slots, faster potion cooldowns, stronger potions and a few more bag slots.
- **A cleaner look:** a simpler hero card (click your portrait to change your avatar), new stone menu icons, faces for the people behind the merchant's counter, a clearer **Build here** window that explains what each building gives, tooltips that disappear when they should, and a grey look for monsters that are still placeholders.
- **Smoother loading:** your bag and crafting bag are ready the moment the game opens, with no empty flash.
- **Crafting is a long road:** crafting is an end-game system, so for now every craft gives much less crafting XP.
- **Under the hood:** refreshed pictures everywhere, removed leftover developer tools, and a few fixes.
