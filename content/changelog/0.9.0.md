# 0.9.0 - Crafting
date: 2026-11-19

- **Crafting lists every kind of item with six tiers.** Weapons are split into **swords, daggers, axes, maces, mauls, spears, bows, crossbows, staves and wands**, off hands into **shields and tomes**, and every armor and jewelry slot has its own group. Each one has six tiers (Starter, Hide, Iron, Steel, Knight, Obsidian), unlocking at crafting level 1, 8, 15, 25, 35 and 50.
- **A recipe makes only its own kind of piece.** A club no longer shows a wand's spell power: a sword recipe rolls a sword, a bow recipe a bow, and the stats shown under the odds are the ones that type can have.
- **Refined materials.** Smelt ore into **Copper, Iron, Steel, Silver and Gold Ingots** and the **Obsidian Edge**, weave **Cloth Bolts**, press **Herb, Fungal and Vital Extracts**, cut **Stone Blocks**. Weapons, armor and cloaks from the Hide tier up now use them.
- **Nails and rivets for armor.** Hammer ingots into **Copper Nails, Iron Nails, Steel Rivets, Silver Rivets and Gold Studs**; every armor recipe from the Hide tier up is fastened with the ones of its metal.
- **Polish your own gems.** Every rough gem has its own polishing recipe (Jasper, Malachite, Turquoise, Geode, Amethyst, Lapis, Garnet, Moonstone, Crystal, Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, Heart Gem). Polishing needs tools you craft too: **Gem Grit** (from flint) for every gem, a **Polishing Cloth** for the blue gems and finer, and a vial of paste for the best. Rings, amulets and charms from the Hide tier up are set with cut gems.
- **More to find in rocks.** Rocks now also drop **Flint**, **Iron Nodules**, **Jasper**, **Malachite**, **Turquoise**, **Lapis**, **Garnet**, **Rough Ruby** and **Rough Sapphire**. The finer the gem, the rarer the rock that holds it and the fewer you get.
- **New potions.** **Mana Potion** and **Vitality Tonic** (heals over time), plus six timed elixirs: **Might** (damage), **Haste** (attack speed), **Ironskin** (armor), **Fortitude** (max health), **Keen Eye** (crit chance) and **Warding** (dodge). Elixirs are drunk by hand from the bag; a second one of the same kind refreshes the first.
- **Two more craft slots from the Ember shop.** **Extra Craft Slot** adds a queue slot for **50** and another for **200 Embers**, up to 5 batches at once (3 at the start).
- **The recipe list is wider** so a whole row of tiers fits.
- **Four monsters get their real pictures:** the **Pinecone Warden** (forest), the **Cairn Sentinel** (hills, now a watchtower with a blue fire), the **Millstone Golem** (fields) and the swamp's **Bogfire Wisp**, which is now the **Sludge Cyclops**. They no longer show the black silhouette.
- **Monsters grow twice as fast.** A monster on the map now gains a level every **15 minutes** instead of every 30, so it reaches its growth cap in about 2.5 hours. Monsters already on the map catch up when this update lands.
- **One slot per kind of junk.** Junk materials no longer stack to 100,000: a single bag slot holds up to a trillion of one kind, so a pile of rubble or scrap is one slot instead of many. Stacks you already have are merged into one the next time the game saves.
- **Faster, longer and cheaper crafting.** Three new Ember shop upgrades: **Workbench** (+0.25 craft speed per level, 4 levels), **Night Shift** (+8 hours a batch runs without you per level, 3 levels) and **Thrifty Hands** (-3% of the resources every craft takes per level, 5 levels). Three one-time Gem shop items: **Master Workbench** (+50% speed), **Overnight Orders** (+24 hours of batch run) and **Bulk Discount** (-10% resources). Discounts never go past 25% in total, and only apply to resources (not to components or ingredients). A batch keeps the discount it was paid with, so cancelling refunds exactly what you paid.
- **Two more tiers: Runed (G7) and Ancient (G8).** Every weapon, armor and jewelry type now has eight tiers; Runed unlocks at crafting level 65 and Ancient at 80. They can only be crafted: no monster, boss or chest drops them and the merchant never sells them. They need the finest metals (Obsidian Edges, Gold Ingots, Gold Studs) and cut gems.
- **The biome materials finally matter.** Herbs, Sap, Iron Ore, Spores, Frostshard, Pearls and Salt from your Extractors are now used in: **crafting** (Iron grade gear and above take them: weapons Iron Ore and Sap, armor Salt and Spores, jewelry Pearls and Frostshard, cloaks Herbs and Salt; ingots, extracts, elixirs and the finest gems too), **Producer and Extractor upgrades** (from level 3: 1,000, 5,000, 25,000, then x5) and the **castle** (every upgrade from level 6 to 7 on eats one of them: herbs for level 7, sap for 8, iron ore for 9, spores for 10, then frostshard, pearls and salt). The merchant still buys them for 5 gold each, but they are worth far more as crafting inputs.
- **The Crafting bag has actions.** Click a stack to open its menu: take a stack or everything that fits for potions and goods, or **Craft with it...** for a material, which opens the Crafting tab searched for it (Shift+click still takes everything at once). Ingredients are coloured by tier (grey, green, blue, purple) and their tooltip says what uses them and where they drop. They can't be sold or taken out.
- **Elixir of Time Shift (coming together).** The most expensive brew in the game: drinking one moves the clock forward an hour, paying an hour of income (at your away efficiency) and taking an hour off the castle and building timers; up to 3 a day. The recipe unlocks at crafting level 70 and needs 5 **Hourglass Sand**, a rare drop of the Banner Wraith and the Cinder Warlord.
- **Enchant craft (locked).** A piece of gear in your bag can have **one affix rerolled or one added** (up to what its rarity allows) at the workbench, for Ichor. Ichor is made at the workbench from the Blight's Corrupted Core.
- **The three legendary weapons can be crafted** (Dawnsplitter, Stormstring, Starlight Wand): from crafting level 80, 50 War Medals each, one copy at a time and only with a free bag slot. War Medals are made at the workbench from the Battlefield's Scrap Steel and a Gold Ingot.
- **More builders from the Gem shop.** "Builder #2" is now the **Additional builder**, bought in three levels (2,000, 6,000 and 12,000 gems, 20,000 in all). Each level adds one more castle or building upgrade running at the same time, up to 5 builders together with the Ember shop's Extra Builder. If you already bought Builder #2 you own level 1.
- **Rings hit harder.** From the Bronze tier (G3) up, rings carry flat **damage** and more **crit** instead of mostly health, and the Sharp / of Power damage affix can now roll on them. The first two tiers keep their old rings so the early bosses stay as they were. (Attack speed is a weapon trait and is not a ring stat.)
- **Gear keeps scaling with item level.** Above item level 55 the flat stats of a piece (damage, armor, health) now also compound by 7% for every level, so the late game is no longer flat: a Runed axe hits for about 200, an Ancient one for 700 to 1,600, and a Legendary one for thousands, where an Iron axe hits for 30. Bosses that scale with gear grow the same way, so fights stay fair. Crit, dodge and other percentages grow as before, so their caps are not rushed.
- **Every biome has its buildings.** Jungle, Fields, Blossom Orchard, Blight and Battlefield now have a **Producer** and an **Extractor** like the home biomes (Canopy Camp and Venom Press, Windmill Farmstead and Dye Works, Blossom Yard and Royal Apiary, Rot Works and Core Siphon, War Camp and Scrap Forge). The Extractors make five new materials: **Venom**, **Dye**, **Royal Jelly**, **Corrupted Core** and **Scrap Steel**, used like the other extracts in upgrades and crafting and sold to the merchant for 5 gold.
- **Ichor and War Medals have a source.** **Distilled Ichor** (from Corrupted Core, crafting level 60) unlocks the enchant craft, and **Forged War Medals** (from Scrap Steel and a Gold Ingot, crafting level 70) unlock the three legendary weapons. Both are made at the workbench in the Materials list.
- **More than one of each building.** You can now build **two, three or four** copies of every Producer and Extractor (Idols stay one per map). The Ember shop sells **Building Slots** (two levels: +1 copy for 80 and +1 for 240 Embers) and the Gem shop one **Building Slot** (6,000 gems, +1 copy); all of them together make 4. Every copy makes its own output and has its own levels.
- **Six general buildings.** The **Watchtower** (+damage), **Marketplace** (+gold from selling), **Training Yard** (+XP), **Scriptorium** (+crafting XP), **Guild Hall** (+gold and XP from kills) and **Garrison** (+max HP and armor) stand on any land except the lake. Each level of each copy adds 1% of its effect; you can build 3 Watchtowers and Marketplaces and 2 of each of the others, on top of the Producers, Extractors and Idols.
- **Gems and ore in chests.** Every chest can now also hold ore (Copper, Flint, Iron) and rough gems (Jasper, Malachite, Turquoise, then Garnet and Lapis, and very rarely Ruby and Sapphire), sent straight to the Crafting bag. The better the chest level, the finer the finds. The monsters of the rebirth biomes drop finer stones and ores too.
- **Herb Crates and Trade Packs.** Two new goods sell to the merchant and give the surplus of your Extractors a market: the **Herb Crate** (Herbs and Sap, from crafting level 20) and the **Trade Pack** (Iron Ore, Salt and Pearls, from level 32).
- **Resource tooltips show their tier.** The top-bar resource tooltips are coloured and labelled by tier (base material, farm product, biome good, extract), like the Crafting bag ingredients.
- **The Build here window has two columns and a fixed order.** Producer, Extractor, Idol, then the general buildings always stay in the same place, also when one of them is built elsewhere and offers "Move here".
- **General buildings explain themselves.** The Scriptorium, Watchtower and the others now show "Its bonus" (for example +1 % crafting XP now, +2 % at the next level) and how their copies add up, instead of a land bonus of +0 %.
- **Clearer Rebirth screen.** The button now reads "Rebirth & earn N Embers", the note explains that the permanent income bonus grows with every Ember you have ever earned (so more tiles and a higher castle raise both) and that the castle bonus is separate. Pack Rat now says its bag slots are kept through rebirth.
- **Hourglass Sand drops.** The Banner Wraith (rare) and the Cinder Warlord (always) drop Hourglass Sand, so the Elixir of Time Shift can be crafted. Venom, Dye and Royal Jelly now say honestly that nothing uses them yet.
- **The castle window saves your attention while it builds.** During an upgrade the cost you already paid is shown greyed as "Current upgrade, already paid", and the next upgrade is the main panel with its green and red costs, so you can see what to save for.
- **Small income rates are readable.** The merchant ledger shows income per second with two decimals (for example +0.38/s) and "<0.01/s" for tiny ones, instead of rounding farm products down to +0/s.
- **Elixir of Arcana.** A new elixir (crafting level 26: Arcane Essence, Glowberry, a Glass Vial and Herbs) that gives **+50 % mana regeneration for 3 minutes**, so the Stun and Heal spells come back sooner.
- **A calmer buff row.** With more than three potion effects running, the combat window folds them into one "Buffs (N)" line that opens on a click.
- **A fuller Kingdom overview.** Land now shows each biome's share of your tiles (for example 21.7 %). Buildings are grouped by biome, always in the order Producer, Extractor, Idol, then the general buildings, and a building of a biome you have not unlocked yet says "Locked: rebirth N" instead of "Not built". The count reads "built of available". "Monsters on the map" also counts golden monsters, fully grown and still growing monsters, unopened chests and ore nodes.
- **Compare a ring with either ring you wear.** Hovering a ring in the bag or at the merchant compares it with the first free ring slot; hold **Ctrl** to compare it with your other ring. The list of changes says which ring it replaces ("Difference: replacing Ring 2"). Double-click still equips into the first free ring slot.
- **One place for the changes.** Item cards no longer put green or red brackets beside every stat or print a separate "Properties lost when equipped" list; the Difference section lists every change once, gains first and losses after.
- **Readable stat names.** Item cards and the Stats tab spell stats out (Crit Chance, HP Regen, Mana Regen, Spell Power, Attack Speed, Strength) instead of CRIT CH, HP REG, MP REG, SPELL PWR, ATK SPD and STR.
