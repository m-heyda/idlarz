# 0.8.0 - The Hall of Deeds
date: 2026-11-18

- **The Hall of Deeds is a full window.** Deeds are grouped in **categories** (Combat, Bestiary, Realm, Explorer, Economy, Crafting, Gear, Hero, Farm, Rebirth, Special, Secrets), with a **tag filter** and three ways to sort: claimable first, closest to the next tier, or by category. Pick a deed to read its story, the tier ladder with what each tier gives, how to make progress and any **special unlocks** (titles and avatars) it holds.
- **Tier diamonds:** every deed shows a yellow diamond for each tier you have claimed. On the light paper detail the diamonds are violet, one per tier. Claimed milestones and tiers get a green tick. A pulsing diamond is a tier waiting for you to claim it. Deeds without their own picture yet show a **medal silhouette**.
- **Points first, gems on top.** The list shows the deed points of a tier. Claiming also pays gems: **50 for the first tier of every deed and 25 more for each further tier**. The Early and Beta tester deeds keep their own gem rewards.
- **Deed points count when you claim.** Earning a tier gives nothing until you press **Claim**; only claimed tiers add to your deed points.
- **Many more deeds:** lifetime income deeds for gold, wood, stone, coal, food, meat, every farm product (eggs, milk, hide, wool) and every extract (herbs, sap, iron ore, spores, frostshards, pearls, salt); **Crafting mastery** by crafting level; **Epic forger** and **Legendary forger** for gear you craft yourself; **Epic wardrobe** and **Legendary wardrobe** for gear you wear; **Ember keeper** for the Embers you earn over all rebirths; **Sore loser** for fights you lose; and more tiers for **Golden touch** and **Bossbane**.
- **On the podium:** reach first, second or third place on any Ranking board for a bronze, silver and gold deed (and the title **Champion**). The game notices when you look at the Rankings.
- **Secret deeds:** a few new ones to find on your own, some of them silly. They show as ??? until you earn them.
- **Early tester and Beta tester for everyone:** every account that exists today now holds the **Early tester** and **Beta tester** deeds, with their avatars and titles.
- **Much longer ladders:** **Slayer** now goes up to 5 million monsters, **Hoarder** up to **1 trillion gold** held at once, **Market Day** up to 10 billion units sold, plus more tiers for Apprentice Smith, Reborn, Untouched and Wolfsbane.
- **New titles** to wear next to your name, among them Slayer, Bane of Beasts, Dragon's Hoard, Grand Merchant, Grandmaster Smith, Eternal Phoenix, Boss Hunter, Worldbreaker, Midas, Gold Baron, Ember Lord, Legend Smith, Artisan and Champion.
- **Deed milestones:** reach 50, 150, 300, 600, 1,000, 1,500, 2,250, 3,000, 4,500 and 6,000 deed points and claim each one for **gems** and a small permanent bonus: **+1% gold** or **+1% XP from kills**, alternating, up to +5% of each. Your active bonus is shown at the top of the window, with a bar to the next milestone.
- **Claim all always works,** even when only one reward is waiting. It also collects the milestones those points reach, in the same click. The dock icon counts tiers and milestones waiting.
- **Deeds in the Rankings:** the Deeds tab is open. It ranks the players with the most deed points from **claimed** tiers (ties go to whoever got there first), and it is rebuilt once a day like the other boards.
- **Rankings fix:** the Tiles tab now shows the arrows for places you moved up or down, instead of marking everyone as new.
- **Kingdom overview:** a land heatmap, buildings in two columns, and a purple upgrade bar on tiles that are being upgraded.
- **Item tooltips** now say what an item is when it has no equipment slot: **Junk**, **Consumable** or **Crafting material**.
- **Crafting ingredients:** the rarer things monsters leave are no longer junk. **Vial of Blood, Wet Clay, Dried Herb Bundle, Orange Caps, Wild Grapes, Hollow Log, Glowberry, Dark Horn, Copper Ore, Ember Coal, Obsidian Chip, Geode, Dark Shard, Murky Phial, Moonstone Sliver, Fire Drop, Emerald Star and Heart Gem** now drop straight into your **Crafting bag**, cannot be sold, and say in their tooltip where they drop and what they are planned for (potions, ingots, planks, jewelry). No recipe uses them yet; they are the start of the stock the next crafting updates will build on. The cheap drops (rubble, bones, feathers, bacon and the like) stay junk for the merchant. Stacks you already hold in your bag stay sellable junk. The Bestiary marks which drops are ingredients.
- **Bestiary drop tables are grouped:** **Rewards** (gold, XP, gear, potions), **Junk** and **Crafting ingredients**, with each ingredient coloured by its tier (grey, green, blue, purple).
- **Simpler item tooltips:** stats are grouped under two headings, **Combat** (damage, defense, mana) and **Economy** (income, luck, finds), instead of four.
- **Merchant trades:** a save made just before a trade can no longer make the game forget your gold and refuse your next sale or purchase, and a purchase the browser lost no longer blocks your buying.
