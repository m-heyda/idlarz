# 0.8.0 - The Hall of Deeds
date: 2026-11-18

- **The Hall of Deeds is a full window.** Deeds are grouped in **categories** (Combat, Bestiary, Realm, Explorer, Economy, Crafting, Gear, Hero, Farm, Rebirth, Special, Secrets), with a **tag filter** and three ways to sort: claimable first, closest to the next tier, or by category. Pick a deed to read its story, the tier ladder with what each tier gives, how to make progress and any **special unlocks** (titles and avatars) it holds.
- **Tier diamonds:** every deed shows a yellow diamond for each tier you have claimed, and the tier ladder lights one up per tier. A pulsing diamond is a tier waiting for you to claim it. Deeds without their own picture yet show a **medal silhouette**.
- **Points first, gems on top.** The list shows the deed points of a tier. Claiming also pays gems: **50 for the first tier of every deed and 25 more for each further tier**. The Early and Beta tester deeds keep their own gem rewards.
- **Deed points count when you claim.** Earning a tier gives nothing until you press **Claim**; only claimed tiers add to your deed points.
- **Much longer ladders:** **Slayer** now goes up to 5 million monsters, **Hoarder** up to **1 trillion gold** held at once, **Market Day** up to 10 billion units sold, plus more tiers for Apprentice Smith, Reborn, Untouched and Wolfsbane.
- **New titles** to wear next to your name: Slayer, Bane of Beasts, Hoarder, Dragon's Hoard, Merchant Prince, Grand Merchant, Master Smith, Grandmaster Smith, Reborn, Eternal Phoenix, Landlord, Wolfsbane, Boss Hunter, Fully Kitted and The Untouchable.
- **Deed milestones:** reach 50, 150, 300, 600, 1,000, 1,500, 2,250, 3,000, 4,500 and 6,000 deed points and claim each one for **gems** and a small permanent bonus: **+1% gold** or **+1% XP from kills**, alternating, up to +5% of each. Your active bonus is shown at the top of the window, with a bar to the next milestone.
- **Claim all always works,** even when only one reward is waiting. It also collects the milestones those points reach, in the same click. The dock icon counts tiers and milestones waiting.
- **Deeds in the Rankings:** the Deeds tab is open. It ranks the players with the most deed points from **claimed** tiers (ties go to whoever got there first), and it is rebuilt once a day like the other boards.
- **Rankings fix:** the Tiles tab now shows the arrows for places you moved up or down, instead of marking everyone as new.
- **Kingdom overview:** a land heatmap, buildings in two columns, and a purple upgrade bar on tiles that are being upgraded.
- **Item tooltips** now say what an item is when it has no equipment slot: **Junk**, **Consumable** or **Crafting material**.
- **Merchant trades:** a save made just before a trade can no longer make the game forget your gold and refuse your next sale or purchase, and a purchase the browser lost no longer blocks your buying.
