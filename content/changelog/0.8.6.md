# 0.8.6 - Mining
date: 2026-11-18

- **Rocks and ores on the map.** Rocks now appear on your land, much more often than chests (about one every two and a half minutes, faster with Luck). They never stand on water, buildings, monsters or chests, and the map holds a limited number at a time that grows with your land. The server decides where they appear and how rare they are, and **Luck** makes the better kinds likelier.
- **Five kinds of rock:** **Rock** (50 clicks to break), **Silver ore** (125), **Rich ore** (250, a lump of gold), **Crystal** (500) and the very rare **Legendary ore** (1,000 clicks, a dark rock veined with fire). The Legendary ore gives the most stone and coal, the best ores often and, now and then, **Ember Coal**. Every rock has a soft glow in its rarity colour and a dark outline, so it stands out on grass, snow and sand.
- **Mine with a pickaxe.** The cursor turns into a pickaxe over a rock, and the pickaxe **swings** with every click. Each click chops it with a short **mining sound** (it plays fast, with a little variety) and a small nudge of the rock, and a bar under it fills as you go. Hover a rock to see its rarity and the **clicks left**. The progress is kept on the server, so it survives a reload, and the server only lets in as many clicks per second as a person can make.
- **What a rock gives:** **stone and coal** (a share of your income, more from better rocks) and, at a low chance, **crafting ingredients** that go straight into the Crafting bag: **Copper Ore** from any rock, **Silver Ore**, **Gold Ore** and **Raw Crystal** from the better ones, and a rare **Ember Coal** (1 to 3). The new ores are crafting ingredients for later recipes and cannot be sold yet.
- **A broken rock shows what it dropped.** A burst of chips flies out, and the stone, coal and ores it really gave land in a row with their amounts under them, stay for a second and fade away. One notice at the top lists everything the rock gave.
- **Highlight buttons under the minimap.** Three small buttons, **Highlight chests**, **Highlight ores** and **Highlight monsters**, ring every one of that kind on the map with a pulsing marker, and a small arrow on the edge of the screen points to the nearest one that is out of view. The buttons are small, centred under the minimap, and highlight one kind at a time; the choice is remembered, and the markers lie under the tooltips.
- **Ores on the minimap.** Rocks and ores show as **purple dots** (grouped when they are close together), with their count under the minimap.
- **Mining deeds** in the Hall of Deeds: **Rock breaker**, **Silver miner**, **Ore hoarder**, **Crystal cracker**, **Legend of the mine** and **Ember Coal finder**, each with five tiers (the top ones come with a title). The server counts every rock you break.
- **Click gold fix.** A click made at exactly the sustained clicking pace could be refused because of a rounding error, and two different clicks in some browsers could be mistaken for one, so gold per click sometimes seemed to stop until a reload. Both are fixed. Clicks on a rock, a monster or a chest are still pure actions and pay no click gold.
- **Rock counter fix.** The clicks a rock needs now always come from the server's answer, so a game that loaded just before an update never shows a wrong count.
- **Old ingredients move to the Crafting bag.** Stacks of **Vial of Blood, Wet Clay, Dark Horn** and the other ingredients that you picked up before the Crafting bag existed were still sitting in your bag as junk. The server now moves them into the Crafting bag the next time you play (what does not fit stays where it is), so every ingredient is in one place and none is sold by accident with Sell junk.
