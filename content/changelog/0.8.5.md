# 0.8.5 - Boss mechanics and attribute gear
date: 2026-11-18

- **Boss modifiers:** biome bosses now fight differently. **Venom** poisons you with every hit that lands, stacking up to a limit: each stack drains a share of your max HP every second and cuts your regeneration while it lasts. **Sluggish** makes your potions take longer to come off cooldown. **Enrage** makes the boss attack faster every few seconds of the fight. **Brutal** makes every hit land harder. Each boss carries one to three of them, shown as **chips** in the boss banner (hover one for the numbers), and poison ticks show as green numbers. The Great Auroch has none.
- **Auto-battle knows about them:** its win estimate counts poison, enrage, brutal and slower potions, so it does not pick fights it would lose to them.
- **Attributes on gear:** **Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Vitality and Charisma** can now roll as prefixes and suffixes on gear, on the pieces that suit them. Worn attribute gear adds to the points you spent, and your damage, crit, attack speed, armor, max HP and mana, Heal, Luck and XP gain all follow. The Character window shows how much of each attribute comes from gear.
- **Crafting gear:** **Intelligence** gear improves the quality and affix values of the gear you craft. Put it on, let the game sync, then claim your craft. The crafting window shows your Intelligence and how much comes from gear.
- **Rebirth biomes spawn further out:** Jungle now needs at least 10 tiles from the castle, Fields 14, Blossom Orchard 18, Blight 22 and Battlefield 22. They get likelier the further out you go, so you find them in patches instead of next to your castle.
- **Swamp and Tundra come later:** Swamp unlocks after 12 purchased tiles (was 6) and needs 6 tiles from the castle; Tundra after 25 (was 10) and 10 tiles out.
- **Idols cost much more material from level 3:** Idol levels 3, 4 and 5 now ask for **10,000, 20,000 and 40,000** of their biome's extract (it was 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000).
- **Name, badge and title window** is wider, so the longer titles fit on one line.
- **Farm upgrades:** the **Not enough** button stays on one line, and long cost lists wrap under the text instead of squeezing it.
