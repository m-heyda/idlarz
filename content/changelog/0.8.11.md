# 0.8.11 - Spawn rebalance
date: 2026-11-19

- **Rocks are rarer.** The map now holds **one rock per 100 tiles** you own (it was one per 50), so 503 tiles hold up to 5.
- **More monsters, a few less chests.** A new monster appears every **10 minutes** (it was 15) and a chest every **12 minutes** (it was 10).
- **Luck now speeds up everything that appears on your map.** Chests, rocks, monsters and bosses all come sooner with more Luck, and more strongly than before: up to **twice as fast** at 167 Luck.
